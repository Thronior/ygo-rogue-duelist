Relic changes

Tribute FreeREWORKED

Level 7 or higher monsters you Tribute Summon require 1 less Tribute.

Before: Level 7 or higher monsters you Tribute Summon require only 1 Tribute.

What changed: Level 7 and 8 monsters still need just 1 Tribute, and the Egyptian Gods (and other 3-Tribute monsters) now need 2 instead of 3.

Titan's CovenantREWORKED

Level 5 or higher monsters you control gain 1,200 ATK. Level 7 or higher monsters you Tribute Summon require 1 less Tribute.

Before: Level 7 or higher monsters you Tribute Summon require only 1 Tribute.

What changed: Same as Tribute Free: the Egyptian Gods now need only 2 Tributes.

Living TrapsREWORKED

At the start of the Duel, add 1 random Trap Monster card from your Deck to your hand. Trap Monsters you control gain 600 ATK/DEF and cannot be destroyed by your opponent's Spell/Trap effects. The first time each Duel you Special Summon a Trap Monster, you may Set a different Trap Monster directly from your Deck.

Before: Trap Monsters you control gain 600 ATK/DEF and cannot be destroyed by battle. The first time each Duel you Special Summon a Trap Monster, you may Set a different Trap Monster directly from your Deck.

What changed: You start with a Trap Monster in hand, and the protection moved from battle to your opponent's Spells and Traps.

Level Up ChainFIX

Start each Duel with a random Level 1–4 LV monster from your Deck and a copy of "Level Up!" in your hand. Your LV monsters gain 500 ATK and are unaffected by your opponent's monster effects.

Before: ...are unaffected by monster effects.

What changed: It also blocked your LV monsters' own effects (Silent Magician got no counters). Now only your opponent's monster effects are blocked.

Toon PremiereNEW

At the start of each Duel, place 1 "Toon World" from your Deck face-up in your Spell & Trap Zone.

What changed: A new relic for the new Toon support in Pegasus's packs. No LP to pay.

Toon KingdomNEW

At the start of each Duel, place 1 "Toon World" from your Deck face-up in your Spell & Trap Zone. "Toon World" you control cannot be destroyed by card effects. Toon monsters you control gain 500 ATK and can attack the turn they are Summoned.

What changed: A new fused relic. Find out what fuses into it yourself.

Spell ProviderQUALITY OF LIFE

Once per turn, during your Standby Phase, you can place 1 Spell Counter on a card you control that can hold a Spell Counter.

What changed: When only one card can take the counter, it goes there by itself. With several, you still choose.

Spell Counter EngineQUALITY OF LIFE

During your Standby Phase, place up to 2 Spell Counters on cards you control that can hold them. Monsters you control gain 200 ATK for each Spell Counter on your field.

What changed: Same as Spell Provider: no question when only one card can take the counters.

Unstable DNAQUALITY OF LIFE

At the start of each Duel, select one Type and one Attribute. All of both players' monsters everywhere become the selected Type and Attribute. Monsters you control gain 100 ATK for every face-up monster on the field with the selected Type and Attribute.

What changed: The picker opens with last Duel's Type and Attribute already selected.

Reversed RemedySHADOW RELIC

Each time either player would take effect damage or pay LP, they gain that much LP instead. Each time either player would gain LP during a Duel, they take that much damage instead.

What changed: Offered less often. Its text is unchanged.

Stars AlignedFIX

Once per turn, during your End Phase, if you have at least 1 monster in your GY and all monsters in your GY and on your field have the same Level: Special Summon 1 monster of that Level from your hand.

What changed: In Draft Duels it summoned from and to the wrong side for the second player. It and 13 other relics now work for whoever owns them, and "both players" relics hit both.

Fused archetype relicsREWORKED

Fused relics that took the place of an archetype relic keep its shop bonus: that archetype's cards show up far more often in the shop.

What changed: They used to lose it, so a fused relic could leave you with fewer of your deck's cards to buy.

Green text is new. Fused relics you have discovered now show which fusion each piece belongs to.
