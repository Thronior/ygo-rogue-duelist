Update 0.2.43

Opponents

Johnny Steps

New Tier 1 duelistKyky's Fusion and Equip deck: Musician King, Empress Judge, Fusion Weapon and Re-Fusion.

New cards

The Moth LV line

Moth LV1, LV2, LV6, LV8and Cocoon of Evolution - S: a Moth that levels up each Standby Phase. They join every pack the original Moths are in.

Relics

New relics

Sphinx relicsPyramid of Lightning, Bond of Egypt and Shadow of Anubis, plus the fused relic Chimera Sphinx.

Insect relicsMoth Brood leaves a Moth Token behind your Insects, plus the fused relic Insect Swarm. Chitin Carapace now gives 800 DEF.

Sealed FateYour Destiny Board and Spirit Message cards cannot be targeted by card effects.

Relic tweaksRestless Dead now works in your opponent's Standby Phase. Narrow Gate now closes the outer zones.

Controls

Drag to play, smoother

Drop a Trap on your field and it's Set. A card with only one way to play just plays. The card waits on its zone while you choose, and you can drag cards along your hand to reorder it. Any hand card can be picked up and played with.

Art

New portraits

38 character portraits replaced with new ones, and every character pack rebuilt with them.

Main menu

Completion

The title screen shows how complete your game is, counted from your achievements.

Settings

New settings

Spin/Slots Effects: turn off the spinning reels. Original Japanese Names for duelists (cards keep theirs). Auto-Pick Single Choices, on by default. Auto-Placement is now on by default.

Deck editor

Auto-fill

Short of the minimum? The grey arrow next to the card count shows which cards would fill your deck, and adds them with one tap.

Tag duels

Trade in the shop

The Trade button now works in the shop's deck editor. Shopping waits while a trade is open. Rooms no longer say they're full when you were the one who joined.

Draft

See your deck and relics

You can view your deck while you pick cards and while you wait for your opponent. The waiting room shows both players' names and relics, and the Draft deck editor shows your relics too.
