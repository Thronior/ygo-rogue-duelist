YGO Rogue Duelist

Update 0.2.37

Important fixes

Tag rooms, big runs and saves

Tag said rooms were full once 256 existed. There is no limit now, and rooms nobody is in are cleared after 10 minutes.

Runs with more than 10,000 cards said "This slot has no valid saved run." They load again.

Windows app: "Local save operation failed" when Documents is synced with OneDrive. Fixed.

Long heal and burn chains (Fire Princess builds) no longer freeze the duel.

Cursed relics

Two new cursed relics

Mulligan's OfferOnce per Duel, at your first Standby Phase: shuffle any number of hand cards into your Deck and draw that many. You can't add cards from your Deck to your hand except by drawing.

Graceful & Skull DiceThe first die you roll each turn: gain result ×100 LP. Your face-up monsters lose result ×100 ATK this turn.

Deck editor

Marks, everywhere

The card window now has −1/3+ under the art, with the card's mark below it:

☆★✕

New Marked first option in the Deck status menu. ★ now helps cards that aren't in your Deck yet; ✕ only greys out and sorts last.

Duels

Relics first, no suicide attacks

Your relic triggers always resolve first, so a forced window no longer makes you use a card instead.

The CPU no longer attacks into a loss to trigger Sangan, Witch, Mystic Tomato and the like. It Sets Sangan and Witch.

CPU opponents

Rock, Toon and Zombie decks

New CPU rules for Order to Charge, Silent Doom, Heart of the Underdog, Dust Barrier, Toon Defense, Toon Tributes, Giant Axe Mummy, Vampire's Curse and Metamorphosis.

Aknadin's Tier 3 deck is now a Rock / Earth deck.

Draft Duels

Cleaner drafts

Copycat's first loadout and both Dueling Engine loadouts are greyed out; Copycat plays as the Randomizer.

Relics that only work outside duels (coins, shop, deck rules) left the Draft relic pool; the two new cursed relics joined it.
