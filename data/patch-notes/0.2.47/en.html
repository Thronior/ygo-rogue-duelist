Changes

  • 🏹An attack arrow stays on the board until the attack ends.
  • 🔍Collection › Packs: search a card name to find the packs that hold it. Pack windows list every Type and a Staples filter.
  • 📊Deck editor: optional Attribute and Type counts (Settings › Display).
  • 🗑️Free Duels: delete custom decks.
  • 🏆Ultimate Collector: Unready while siding, Limited marks on your deck, and Monster / Spell / Trap counts.
  • 🪙Tag victories pay 25% more coins.
  • 🔒Relic Seal below Challenge Level 2 seals half of your relics (rounded up).
  • 🎭Name Changer: picking Toon makes them Toon monsters, and its archetype counts for Magician Staff and card previews.
  • 🖨️Faulty Printer also misprints free Random Packs.
  • 🌈Randomized cards: "cannot control more monsters" effects send the extra monsters to the GY.
  • 🔗Link devices: join another device's link anytime; shows the link ID and last sync.
  • 🧠CPU: attacks directly with Jinzo #7 and Toons, and past an unattackable Legendary Fisherman; better Waboku, Negate Attack, Newdoria and Tribute choices.
  • 👆Legion the Fiend Jester's summon choice works with one tap.
  • 🎨Parting Gift has its own art. The Easy button is easier to spot.

Fixes

  • ⚖️The CPU's Obelisk no longer Tributes itself.
  • 🐑Double Spell copying Scapegoat Summons the Sheep Tokens.
  • 🤝Loyalty no longer keeps a monster you took with Enemy Controller.
  • 🔥Hot Deck no longer causes an "effect went wrong" error.
  • ⭐Stars Aligned no longer asks when your field is full.
  • 🩸Ritual Scar shows the right ATK during the Duel.
  • 💾Resuming an old Tag or Draft room no longer rolls back your Challenge Levels.
  • 🃏Roulette Spider and other Quick-Play, Field and Counter cards show their real icons.
  • 🔢The ATK sort works on a new run's first deck screen.
  • 🎲Randomized Standby Phase burn cards can be used, and "…, then …" effects no longer need the second part to be possible.
