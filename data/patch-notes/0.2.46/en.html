Stats

New Stats screens

CPU Stats and Character Stats are now a list you can search and sort (most played, toughest, best win rate...), next to the picked one's page: win rate by Challenge Level, toughest CPUs and every record.

Relic shop

Banishing and rewards

Banishing a relic costs 20 coins, then 10 more each time (up to 60), and its slot stays empty. Champion Trophy's second pick shows 3 fresh relics.
