Language-picker flags, one per interface language. The artwork is from [lipis/flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) v7.2.3 (4x3 set), licensed under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`), shown at 3:2 (30x20) with the 4:3 artwork centred:

- `de` Germany, `es` Spain, `fr` France, `it` Italy, `ja` Japan, `ko` South Korea, `zh` China
- `en` and `pt` are diagonal composites of the United Kingdom/United States and Portugal/Brazil flags.

The SVGs are bundled locally so the picker works offline.
