# May 29, 2005 content expansion

Added 166 cards, 18 booster products, and 10 starter/structure decks.

Both TCG and OCG. Excluded vehicle roids, Elemental HERO and dedicated support; removed their original booster products and cards exclusive to those products. Retained five existing CPU cards by user request.
