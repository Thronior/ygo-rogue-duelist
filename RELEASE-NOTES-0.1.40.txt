## YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.1.40

### Ultimate Collector
- Fixed overlapping reconnect sessions and repeated duel animations.
- Rejoining restores the current duel without replaying its earlier animations.
- Improved recovery from failed timer syncs and interrupted rendering.
- Added an opponent-field overlay when your opponent must respond during your turn.

### CPU fixes
- Improved Axe of Despair recovery decisions to avoid sacrificing valuable monsters.
- Improved targeted control decisions against Spirit Reaper.
- Prevented premature use of Enemy Controller’s battle-position effect.

### Shops, relics and progression
- Toon World adds an extra Toon card to each shop and reroll.
- Legendary Shackles adds an extra Exodia piece to each shop and reroll.
- Golden Ankh now overrides the challenge-level healing cap correctly.
- Removed Phoenix Feather from shops and random starting relics; existing copies remain.
- Added quantity badges for duplicate relics.
- Grouped duplicate cards together within pack-opening rarity groups.
- Rafael now unlocks by completing a run without buying relics.

Available on Android and the webapp.
