Dueling Engine
- Now unlocks by beating Challenge Level 5 with any character. Previous clears count, and existing unlocks are preserved.
- Added View Deck to deck choices, opening a preview over the slots.
- Cards are sorted by Monsters, Spells, and Traps. Inspect cards, close the preview, and continue rerolling normally.

Character Selection
- Made challenge-completion colours more distinct, with matching LVL badges.
- Challenge Level 5 keeps its shiny golden appearance.
- Added a small star to characters that have beaten Challenge Level -1.

CPU Improvements
- Improved Change of Heart and Brain Control decisions to avoid stealing monsters without a useful follow-up.
- Improved consideration of spell immunity and targeting protection.
- Prevented defence-reducing equips from weakening defenders when the ATK boost cannot be used effectively.
- Improved willingness to switch into Attack Position for favourable attacks.

Browser Saves and Offline Downloads
- Added clearer messages when browser storage prevents saving.
- Added options to export current progress and retry saving without repeating the original action.
- Improved startup recovery when saving fails.
- Added storage-space checks and warnings before offline downloads.

UI
- Fixed the broken separator between character names and deck tiers.
- Various minor UI tweaks.

Embedded itch.io screen-rotation improvements are still awaiting verification on a physical phone.
