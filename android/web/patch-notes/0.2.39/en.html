YGO Rogue Duelist

Update 0.2.39

Display styles

Pixel Art, reworked

Cards in duels are now a flat frame in the card's colour with the artwork big inside, easy to read at a glance. The pixel filter is finer and covers the whole screen, while text and flags stay sharp. Cards in the deck builder are no longer pixelated, and card marks are a pixel star and cross .

Display styles

Character select in every look

The character select screen now changes with your display style: Duel Disk gunmetal and gold, Shadow Realm haze, Clean Tournament, Millennium Stone, Kaiba Hologram and Pixel Art (shown above). Change it in Cosmetics.

Relics

Shadow relics and Dark Magic

Cursed relics are now called shadow relics, so they're no longer mixed up with a boss's curses. Nothing else about them changes.

Spell SanctuaryNew shadow relic. You can activate Spell Cards during your opponent's turn. Drawback: you cannot activate Spell Cards unless they are Set first, and you cannot activate a Set Spell Card the turn it was Set.

Immortal Dark MagicFused relic: Magician Staff, Magician's Bond, Master and Apprentice, Black Chaos Rite. Spellcaster monsters you control gain 600 ATK. When you Normal Summon a Spellcaster: you can Special Summon 1 "Dark Magician" monster from your Deck and add 1 card that mentions "Dark Magician" from your Deck to your hand. When a "Dark Magician" monster you control is destroyed and sent to the GY: Special Summon 1 monster that mentions "Dark Magician" from your Deck or Extra Deck, ignoring its Summoning conditions.

Dark SageNow always treated as a "Dark Magician" card, like Summoned Skull is an Archfiend, so every Dark Magician relic counts him.

Yugi Muto's own packLittle Yugi no longer shares Yami Yugi's pack. His 89 cards: Gaia, Black Luster Soldier, Big Shield Gardna, Kuriboh, the Magnet Warriors, Gadgets, the Arcana Knights, LV monsters, Exodia and plenty of draw and removal.

Randomizer

Randomized cards, rebuilt

Rainbow cards are now dealt from the shapes of real cards, so effects, card types and numbers turn up as often as they do on real cards. Cards with archetype effects get the archetype in their name, wording is clearer, contradictory effects are gone and free self-summons from the hand need a cost or condition.

Windows app

The app updates itself

From this version on, the Windows app installs its own updates. If you're on an older version, the main menu shows a notice: download the installer once more, and your saves are kept.

Saving falls back to a second folder if Documents is blocked, and Copy on the run sheet copies the image instead of saving it.

Changes

  • 🌈Starter decks only show up in the shop when you can afford them, and they glow with a rainbow so you can't miss them.
  • 🔎In a pack's details, the Types, Attributes and archetypes are links: tap one to see only that pack's matching cards.
  • 🃏The opponent's deck viewer is as wide as the pack view, showing more cards at once.
  • ⚡When an effect must activate, ✕ is no longer greyed out: pressing it activates the effect.
  • 🈶Character names use their official Japanese, Korean and Chinese names.
  • 🤖The CPU was reviewed against every deck in the game: it now plays dozens of cards it used to ignore, like Amazoness Archers, Catapult Turtle, Reverse Trap and Lady Ninja Yae, and makes better equip and Waboku choices.
  • ↕️Ordering cards on top of the Deck (Big Eye and others) tells you to press ✓ when you're done.

Fixes

  • 📰The in-game patch notes images display correctly again, and every update is now checked before release.
  • 📜Long card text in the card window scrolls again.
  • 🔮Name Changer naming "Dark Magician" now counts for Magician's Bond and Master and Apprentice.
  • 📱The Android app recovers by itself from the rare "Could not start" Python error, and explains what to do if it still can't.
  • 🧠CPU fixes: Prickle Fairy, Cat's Ear Tribe, Legendary Ocean, Serket, Amazoness Swords Woman, Continuous Destruction Punch and flips under Mirror Stats.
  • 🔤The run sheet shows character names in your language.
