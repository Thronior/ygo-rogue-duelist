YGO Rogue Duelist

Update 0.2.40

Cosmetics

74 new sleeves by Deds

Community artist Deds redesigned every Level 6 duelist sleeve and most card sleeves, and made 74 brand-new sleeves, each with its own unlock goal. That's 182 sleeves to collect in Cosmetics, and they load lighter than before.

Languages

Cards in your language

When you play in another language, every real card's picture is now drawn with its official translated name and text, right on the card. English stays as it was.

Tag Duels

Trade requests, bigger shop

The trade screen has a Teammate's cards tab: tap a card to request it, and your partner sees it marked. The Tag shop now offers 2 extra booster packs.

Coins

Bonus coins

An Exodia win now pays at least 150 coins, with no slow-duel penalty.

Hidden coin bonuses: pull off something special in a duel and you may earn coins you didn't know about. Once found, they're listed in the shop's Coin scorecard.

Randomizer

Rainbow cards, polished

Trap Monsters get real stats, empty and unclear texts are fixed (they now say which player), self-revive loops and two engine crashes are gone, and 1,000+ test duels now run clean.

Changes

  • 📊Deck stats include the next boss's curses, so you can build against it.
  • ⚡The deck builder stays smooth even with thousands of cards.
  • 🔍Card search has ★ (marked cards) and ? (randomized cards) filters.
  • ❤️The opponent screen shows your LP next to your coins.
  • ✖️Repeated boss curses show once with ×3.
  • 🪄Magician's Bond can also add "Dark Magician" monsters.
  • 🧟Undying Legion also revives a random Zombie from your GY at the start of your Standby Phase, once per turn.
  • 🛡️Guardian shelves now mostly hold the Guardian cards and their Equips.
  • 🤖The CPU now holds Magic Cylinder and Enchanted Javelin for bigger attackers and stops wasting Zaborg, Rescue Cat and Tokens.

Fixes

  • 📱The Android "Could not start" error on phones set to some languages is fixed.
  • 📜Card windows fit the screen and scroll with UI Scale above 100%, also in Tag.
  • 🃏Related Cards no longer adds a randomized card with the same name.
  • 👆Tapping stacked cards quickly no longer adds copies back.
  • 🧲Name Changer works with Field of Attraction and Twin Eyes.
  • 🌈Character select shows the level colours in every display style.
  • 🔤Unlock goals use the character names of your language.
