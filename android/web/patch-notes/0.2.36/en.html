YGO Rogue Duelist

Update 0.2.36

Important fixes

Loops and German work again

Start Looping crashed from loop 2 on in 0.2.35, so no run could reach loop 3. Fixed.

In German, opening Settings › Display (or picking German there) froze the game, in the Windows app and in browsers. Fixed.

Tag: two players on the same version were told to update if one had opened a Random Pack in solo. Fixed, and a real version mismatch now says whose game is out of date.

Randomized cards

Random Packs go online

TagThe shop can stock a Random Pack (about 1 in 20 shops), and Absolute Random can be found. Both players see the same cards.

Draft DuelsThe Random Pack joins the Draft Screen rotation, next to the character packs.

Clearer effectsRandomized cards roll simpler, tidier effects: fewer costs and actions per effect, and amounts closer to real cards.

In solo shops the Random Pack only takes the second booster slot, never your character pack. Randomized cards are banned in Ultimate Collector: they stay behind when you import a run.

Draft Duels

Second loadouts

Pick a character's second loadout in Draft Duels: you start with its packs and signature cards. Unlocked the same way as in solo (Challenge LVL 2 or LVL -1).

Copycat's Randomizer starts with 4 Random Packs.

Deck editor

Mark your cards

Tap the corner of a card to mark it, in solo and Tag:

☆›★›✕

✕ cards are greyed out and sorted last. Your marks help the shop pick cards for your deck.

Card window

Faster deck building

Add to deck and Remove from deck right in the card window.

Tap a card's Type, Attribute, Level or a card name in its text to search for it.

Main menu

Patch notes and Close

The new bell next to Discord shows every patch notes sheet, and the newest one pops up once after an update.

A ✕ Close button at the top right closes the game (Windows app and Android).

New & changes

  • 🎚️The Relics window shows your run's challenge level and what it does.
  • 🔢Level search: Levels 1-4, 5-6, 7-8, 9-12. Level sort puts the highest ATK first within a Level.
  • 📚Huge collections (9,000+ cards) load fast in the deck editor.
  • 💰More coin tiers: Damage, Effect damage and LP recovered up to 32,000, Largest hit up to 10,000.
  • 🂠Your Set monsters show their face-up ATK/DEF with relic bonuses.
  • 💀The cursed relic screen shows your LP and coins.
  • 👁️Twin Eyes now summons from your hand or Deck.
  • ⛓️Blast with Chain asks in the Damage Step on Chain sometimes.
  • 🖼️Character portrait sizes are normalized.
  • ☁️The main menu tells you when cloud save is paused or couldn't upload.
