# v0.1.93

## Menu and navigation
- Added an Online carousel submenu for Tag Duels, Draft Duels, and Ultimate Collector, with a Main Menu option.
- Used the Unity card artwork for Online and smoothed carousel wrapping so edge cards no longer pop in and out.
- Swapped Collection and Settings positions, clarified the Settings description, and enlarged menu-card descriptions on desktop.
- Added the version number to the main-menu footer.

## Duel feedback
- Direct-attack screen shake and the sword sound now play when direct damage lands.
- LP values pulse and shake when life points change; flipped cards appear face-up as their effects begin.
- Compatibility Mode disables the added animations while retaining existing movement behavior.

## New relic
- Loyalty | 100 coins | Your opponent cannot take control of monsters you control.

## Challenge and curse UI
- Renamed the Challenge Level 2 description to “Amplified Boss Curses.”
- Improved curse icon distinctions and the active curse/relic/level details shown during duels.
