## YGO Rogue Duelist — 0.1.52

- Added menu-card twirls and an enlarged character transition into drafting, keeping the original selection sounds.
- LP and monster stats now count to their new values, with distinct sounds for damage, healing, boosts and reductions. Boosted stats are yellow; reduced stats are red. The existing LP headers now reflect the highest LP reached during the duel.
- Added clearer attack indicators, selected/targeted-card frames, electrical negation feedback, relic activation highlights and deck-ready feedback.
- Fixed equip-target indicators, including monster and Trap equips. Activatable cards in your hand can now be selected directly.
- Improved shop purchase feedback with a coin-counting sound and grayed-out sold cards.
- Bakura now receives four random boosters from the full pack pool, displayed with their actual pack artwork.
- Fixed Draft Duel cancellation, back navigation and reconnect handling. Both players must be present to start, but an interrupted connection no longer prevents the other player from continuing an existing draft or deck edit.
- Added Kemo’s ritual deck progression and the tuned STA02 Tier 2 deck for Leichter.
- Improved animation cleanup and background audio/counter behavior; checked repeated effects and mobile portrait/landscape layouts.
