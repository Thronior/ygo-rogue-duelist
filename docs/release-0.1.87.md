# v0.1.87

This update improves CPU decisions, browser compatibility, shop rewards, and card pool coverage.

- **CPU improvements:** Prioritizes safe lethal attacks, improves Mirror Force retrieval and trap choices, avoids low-value protection plays, uses Skull Dice when it can change the outcome, and makes ritual searchers more useful.
- **Compatibility Mode:** Added a setting that reduces graphics effects that can cause flicker or heavy compositing. Firefox enables it automatically unless the player chooses otherwise.
- **Shop rewards:** Added monster Type and Attribute themes, Field Spells and Support, and Direct Attacks. Route screens now avoid repeating themes and packs; Exodia rewards include a Forbidden One piece.
- **Deck editor:** Newest is the default sort, with Attribute sorting. ATK sorting and displayed monster stats account for applicable relic and misprint changes.
- **Character packs:** Expanded the Odion Character Pack; added 10 cards to the Anubis Character Pack and Stronghold the Moving Fortress to the Yugi Muto Character Pack. Added the linked support cards they need.
- **Booster collections:** Consolidated Premium Packs and Tournament Packs into two collections and broadened character pack card coverage.
- **New cards:** Tiki Curse, Tiki Soul, Tiki Peace, and Swamp Mirrorer.
- **Relics:** Red Medicine now heals 200 LP per full 10 coins held before victory rewards; Merchant Urn now grants 20 coins after each victory.
- **Various minor UI tweaks.**
