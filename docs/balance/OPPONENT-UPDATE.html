Five choices and seven tutorial identities

Tea Gardner, Tristan Taylor, Yugi Muto, Bonz, Espa Roba, Rex Raptor and Mako Tsunami each have a dedicated 40-card beginner deck. Every regular encounter offers five opponents; tag games exclude both players. New Tier 2 and Tier 3 encounters use their corresponding deck versions.

Six shop themes per new opponent

A theme is chosen randomly. Pack rewards use the same weighted card overlap as existing opponents, excluding draft-only packs. Uncovered categories were assigned first where a related strategy or character theme fits. Noah also gained six explicit options, including Spirits.
