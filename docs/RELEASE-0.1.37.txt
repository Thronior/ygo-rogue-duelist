## YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.1.37

### 🎴 Character packs & starting cards
- Added 32 custom character packs, each containing at least 60 cards.
- These characters now start with two of their own packs and two supporting packs that fit their playstyle.
- Engine pieces are more common, while boss monsters and powerful staples are rarer.
- Added missing real-life cards and expanded character signature cards.
- Custom pack artwork now features character portraits, signature-card backgrounds and themed colours.

### 🪙 Shops & boosters
- Added 24 World Championship Tournament 2004 packs to the rotation.
- Shops now offer three booster packs by default.
- New relic: Booster Shelf adds a fourth booster pack.
- New rare relic: Duelist’s Catalogue adds character packs to the shop rotation.
- The expanded regular pack pool can also appear as opponent shop rewards.
- “All X” pack previews now explain their contents without listing every card.
- Cards from the 2004 World Champion deck and the Ultimate Collector Limited list always appear as Ultra Rare in shops and packs. Other rarity rules remain unchanged.

### ✨ Relics, visuals & sound
- Golden Card Sleeve now plays a relic reveal and immediately asks you to choose a card from your current deck, directly over the shop.
- Carbon Copy has a 15% chance to grant an extra copy.
- Added Limited/Semi-Limited icons and sorting by card colour, name, type, ATK, DEF, level and Attribute in Ultimate Collector.
- Challenge selection now uses the same level badges as character portraits.
- Animations are 20% slower, and music/SFX levels have been balanced.

### 🛠️ Fixes & CPU improvements
- Fixed the Flag Behaviour upload error. Failed submissions are saved offline, with a “Send unsent flags” button for retrying them.
- Fixed a CPU attack-cancellation loop that could stall duels.
- Improved CPU handling of The Hunter with 7 Weapons, Penguin Soldier, Solemn Judgment and other card interactions.
- Buying a Starter Deck no longer replaces your equipped deck.
- Shop discounts now apply immediately after purchase.
