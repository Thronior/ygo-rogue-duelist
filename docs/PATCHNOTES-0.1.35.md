**YGO Rogue Duelist — Updates since 0.1.33 (0.1.34–0.1.35)**

**Ultimate Collector**
- New high-stakes multiplayer mode: import a single-player save and build your decks from its collection.
- Best-of-three matches, side decking, ranks, five prize cards for the winner, and permanent elimination for the loser.
- Nine duelist slots, automatic deck saving, match history, and a top-10 surviving-duelist leaderboard with viewable decks.
- Reconnection support, disconnect allowances, turn timers, and new victory/Shadow Realm screens.
- Personal stats retain defeated Collector duelists and their recorded decks.

**Duel and menu improvements**
- Cleaner field-based activation and chain prompts, with highlighted choices and checkmark/cross controls.
- Multiple graveyard, banished, and off-field effects open a selection window when needed.
- Improved Collector layouts for portrait and landscape, rank portraits, collapsible help, and clearer prize selection.

**Gameplay fixes and balancing**
- Yami Yugi, Kaiba, Joey, and Pegasus use their original Starter Decks at Tier 1.
- Shop reroll prices reset after each duel; rerolls replace featured singles with general stock.
- Fixed Waning Star’s level reduction disappearing when the affected monster enters the field.
- Shop discount relics apply immediately after purchase.
- Zombie’s Bargain grants 800 ATK; Cracked Sword now reduces DEF by 1000.
- Longer duels lose 4 reward coins per turn after turn 6.
- Improved CPU decisions for revival, field spells, and several monster effects.
