## YGO Rogue Duelist — 0.1.54

- Fallen Ultimate Collector decks are now bundled with releases from the shared player registry, available to everyone offline. Each UC is a separate opponent using its original player-chosen name and a UC portrait badge.
- Later loops randomly mix regular Tier 4 opponents and bundled UC opponents. Fixed saved UC deck lookups, including Solomon and Copycat.
- Replaced duplicate duel result animations with one longer presentation, with matching defeat styling and the win/loss reason restored.
- Removed the rising-pitch reward landing ding and stopped remaining spin ticks when the reels stop.
- Centered Tap to spin and changed effect-target frames to blue.
- Deck editors now show card images without surrounding boxes or name captions; selection and restriction indicators remain.
- Deck balance is unchanged.
