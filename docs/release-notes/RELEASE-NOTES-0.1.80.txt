v0.1.80

• Added second character variants, unlocked by beating Challenge LVL 2 with that character. Previous completions count automatically. Ryou Bakura, Copycat, and Dueling Engine are excluded for now.
• Updated starting relics, signature cards, supporting packs, and character pack contents to support the new variants.
• Added unique signature-card backgrounds for alternate portraits.

New Relics — Cost → Effect

• Lightning Fire — 70 coins → Pyro and Thunder monsters gain 300 ATK.
• Chitin Carapace — 85 coins → Insect monsters gain 400 DEF.
• Fusion Dividend — 90 coins → Earn 30 coins per Fusion Summon, collected after the duel.
• Swordsman’s Oath — 85 coins → Warrior LV monsters gain 300 ATK.
• Damage Echo — 90 coins → Deal 200 additional damage whenever you inflict effect damage. Cannot trigger itself.
• Counterweight — 90 coins → Your monsters gain 100 ATK per Spell Counter on your field.
• Jar of Echoes — 90 coins → Once during your Standby Phase, change one of your face-up monsters to face-down Defense Position.
• Ritual Incense — 90 coins → Draw a card whenever you Ritual Summon.
• Veteran’s Might — 85 coins → Your Level 4 Normal Monsters gain 200 ATK and DEF.
• Dragon’s Claws — 90 coins → Inflict 300 damage whenever your Dragon destroys an opponent’s monster by battle.
• Armory Crest — 85 coins → Your equipped monsters gain 300 ATK.
