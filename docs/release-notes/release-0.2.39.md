**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.39**

## Display styles
- **Pixel Art, reworked:**
  - Cards in duels are a flat frame in the card's colour with the artwork big inside.
  - The pixel filter is finer and covers the whole screen, while text and flags stay sharp.
  - Deck builder cards are no longer pixelated, and card marks are a pixel star and cross.
- **Character select in every look:** the character select screen now changes with your display style (Duel Disk, Shadow Realm, Clean Tournament, Millennium Stone, Kaiba Hologram, Pixel Art). Change it in Cosmetics.

## Relics
- **Cursed relics are now called shadow relics**, so they're no longer mixed up with a boss's curses. Nothing else about them changes.
- **Spell Sanctuary** (new shadow relic): You can activate Spell Cards during your opponent's turn. Drawback: you cannot activate Spell Cards unless they are Set first, and you cannot activate a Set Spell Card the turn it was Set.
- **Immortal Dark Magic** (fused relic: Magician Staff, Magician's Bond, Master and Apprentice, Black Chaos Rite):
  - Spellcaster monsters you control gain 600 ATK.
  - When you Normal Summon a Spellcaster, you can Special Summon 1 "Dark Magician" monster from your Deck and add 1 card that mentions "Dark Magician" from your Deck to your hand.
  - When a "Dark Magician" monster you control is destroyed and sent to the GY, Special Summon 1 monster that mentions "Dark Magician" from your Deck or Extra Deck, ignoring its Summoning conditions.
- **Dark Sage** is now always treated as a "Dark Magician" card, so every Dark Magician relic counts him.

## Characters
- **Yugi Muto has his own pack** and no longer shares Yami Yugi's. His 89 cards include Gaia, Black Luster Soldier, Big Shield Gardna, Kuriboh, the Magnet Warriors, Gadgets, the Arcana Knights, LV monsters, Exodia, and plenty of draw and removal.
- Character names use their official Japanese, Korean and Chinese names.

## Randomized cards, rebuilt
- Rainbow cards are now dealt from the shapes of real cards, so effects, card types and numbers turn up as often as they do on real cards.
- Cards with archetype effects get the archetype in their name.
- Wording is clearer and contradictory effects are gone.
- Free self-summons from the hand need a cost or a condition.

## Windows app
- From this version on, the Windows app installs its own updates. If you're on an older version, the main menu shows a notice: download the installer once more, and your saves are kept.
- Saving falls back to a second folder if Documents is blocked.
- **Copy** on the run sheet copies the image instead of saving it.

## Changes
- **Starter decks** only show up in the shop when you can afford them, and they glow with a rainbow.
- In a pack's details, the Types, Attributes and archetypes are links: tap one to see only that pack's matching cards.
- The opponent's deck viewer is as wide as the pack view, showing more cards at once.
- When an effect must activate, ✕ is no longer greyed out: pressing it activates the effect.
- The CPU was reviewed against every deck in the game. It now plays dozens of cards it used to ignore, like Amazoness Archers, Catapult Turtle, Reverse Trap and Lady Ninja Yae, and makes better equip and Waboku choices.
- When you put cards back on top of the Deck in the order you choose (Big Eye and others), the game now tells you to press ✓ when you're done.

## Fixes
- The in-game patch notes images display correctly again, and every update is now checked before release.
- Long card text in the card window scrolls again.
- Name Changer naming "Dark Magician" now counts for Magician's Bond and Master and Apprentice.
- The Android app recovers by itself from the rare "Could not start" Python error, and explains what to do if it still can't.
- CPU fixes: Prickle Fairy, Cat's Ear Tribe, Legendary Ocean, Serket, Amazoness Swords Woman, Continuous Destruction Punch, and flips under Mirror Stats.
- The run sheet shows character names in your language.
