**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.46**

## New mode: Free Duels
- Pick any deck, yours or a CPU's, against any CPU deck from every tier, and give relics to both sides. Build your own with + New Deck. Restart or rematch anytime. Nothing is saved. Unlocks once you have won a run.

## Tag Duels
- The host can pick Easy in the Challenge Level list.
- Trades have a Cancel button.
- Two players with the same duelist each get that duelist's pack, and Duelist's Catalogue keeps your partner's pack.

## New relics
- Ra support: Sun's Judgment burns half of every LP you pay, Desert Sunlight heals for your Divine-Beasts, and Seal of the Sun protects them when they're Summoned.
- Sanctuary Hymn: with The Sanctuary in the Sky up, each time your Fairy heals you, your opponent takes the same damage.
- Parasite Infestation: each Duel starts with a face-up Parasite Paracide shuffled into your opponent's Deck.
- Scorching Strikes (shadow relic): your battle damage is halved and dealt as effect damage, so burn relics count it.
- Plus 2 new fused relics to discover. The relic ideas came from your survey answers.

## New cards from your survey
- The Seal of Orichalcos: in Dartz's pack and Nightmare Legion.
- Raiza the Storm Monarch and Caius the Shadow Monarch: in the Paradox Brothers' and Yami Marik's packs, Heaven's Wrath and Nightmare Legion.
- Red-Eyes Wyvern: in Joey's pack and Heaven's Wrath.
- Joey's pack now has removal: Fissure, Trap Hole, Dust Tornado, Sakuretsu Armor, Mystical Space Typhoon and Mirror Force.

## Stats and relic shop
- CPU Stats and Character Stats are now a list you can search and sort, next to the picked one's page: win rate by Challenge Level, toughest CPUs and every record.
- Banishing a relic costs 20 coins, then 10 more each time (up to 60), and its slot stays empty. Champion Trophy's second pick shows 3 fresh relics.

## Changes
- Draft Duels: in a Copycat mirror match, every pack you can pick is a Randomized pack.
- Deck editor (Free Duels and Watch CPU duel): a Save deck button, copies stack as ×2 / ×3, Monster / Spell / Trap counts, and a Custom decks tab showing each deck's first card.
- Free Duels and Watch CPU duel: bigger portraits in a scrolling list.
- CPU: attacks with monsters that destroy what they battle when the trade is worth it, waits for Big Bang Shot to equip, fires Wave-Motion Cannon as soon as it's lethal, and stops attacking into stronger monsters for nothing.
- The split deck view is on by default on screens that fit 10+ cards per row.
- Tag, Draft and the Collector editor sort by Type and Oldest, like solo.
- Randomized cards get a light rainbow tint, and no longer roll effects that undo themselves.
- New Ultra Rare pull sound that fades out softly.
- Insect Swarm and other stat relics preview their boost on cards in your hand and Deck.
- Boss Monster now negates and destroys. Exile Standard counts every banished monster.
- Butterfly Dagger - Elma asks on Chain: Sometimes.
- Umi and Amphibious Bugroth MK-3 left Bandit Keith's pack.
- New portraits for Roland, the Harpie Lady Sisters and Thestalos.
- Import save file is also in Settings › Devices & App.

## Fixes
- Ultimate Collector no longer shows an error when opened with no match in progress.
- Infinite Cards also lifts relic and curse hand limits.
- Conscription Drum only stops monsters that were Normal Summoned, not Flip Summoned ones.
- The CPU no longer wastes Seven Tools of the Bandit on a trap it was already destroying.
- The CPU no longer Summons a monster from Flying Kamakiri #1 or similar cards when that gives you lethal.
- Duel log entries in PvP duels, like Ordeal of a Traveler, are filled in.
- Tag: Exit after a lost run leaves right away.
- Ultimate Collector import explains that runs import between duels.
- Portrait: the shadow relic pick shows its fusion line properly.
