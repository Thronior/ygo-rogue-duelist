**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.1.96**

**Duel & CPU**
- The CPU now uses Exiled Force's effect better.
- The CPU handles their own piercing damage better.
- Opponent phase delays were reduced. The manual response window is configurable in Settings from 0 to 2,000 ms, with a default of 200 ms.

**Damage**
- Fixed damage curse and relic scaling: the normal curse increases damage by 50%, while the amplified version doubles it.

**Character Packs & Cards**
- Updated the Mai Valentine pack, thank you **@Muniqui** for the list.
- Rebuilt the Paradox Brothers pack. Kazejin replaced Monster Gate in the signature cards and is less rare in the pack. Thank you **@Prunk44** for the list and suggestions.
- Added batterymen to the playable catalogue.
- Added Gate Guardian-F as a Rare to all packs featuring Gate Guardian.

**Shop, Settings & Compatibility**
- Prospector’s Lens now adds an extra Rare card to General Stock, including on rerolls.
- Fixed the Android shop’s Relics button so it responds immediately when entering the shop.
- Improved Compatibility Mode for Firefox.
- ATK/DEF sorting in the deck editor keeps monsters with 0 stats above spells and traps.
- Replaced the Field Zone label with a low-opacity field spell icon.

**Additional Duel Update**
- Metalmorph’s “Chain Sometimes” prompt now triggers when a monster is summoned, an attack is declared, or a card is activated.
