*Update Patch Notes*

*New Boss Curses*
• Brittle Guard: Your monsters lose 100 ATK and 200 DEF. Amplified: lose 200 ATK and 400 DEF.
• Thin Ranks: Start with 2 fewer cards (minimum 1). Amplified: 4 fewer cards (minimum 1).
• Open Wound: Take 50% more damage. Amplified: take 100% more damage.
• Withered Hope: Halve LP recovery. Amplified: no LP recovery during the Duel.
• Reversed Might: Swap the original ATK and DEF of all monsters you control. Amplified: affects only your monsters.
• The amplified damage-reduction curse now reduces your battle damage to one-third instead of one-quarter.

*CPU Improvements*
• Avoids attacks that are likely to lose LP without destroying the opposing monster when a better attack is available.
• Avoids wasting monster-removal Spells when all visible opposing monsters are Spell-immune, including Horus LV6.
• Uses Negate Attack against weaker attacks when appropriate, saving Magic Cylinder for stronger attacks.
• Prioritizes attacking unequipped Relinquished before it can use its effect to turn the Duel around.

*Controls and UI*
• Holding the mouse button or screen temporarily enables Always Chain when chain prompts are otherwise set to No Chain. Releasing restores the previous setting.
• Fixed desktop scrolling through card selections in Tag Duels.

*Other Changes and Fixes*
• Collector's Burden now sets the minimum Main Deck size to 50 cards.
• Fixed the run results timeline showing the next selected opponent instead of the opponent just defeated.
