YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.25

Tag Duels play together

You and your partner now pick from one shared list of opponents, and lock in with the swords. You also shop at the same time: Buy, Reroll and Next Duel happen when you both press them. Each boss can be rerolled on its own, and the deck editor opens from the shop.

Bandit Keith: Metalmorph

His second variant is now built around Zoa, Metalmorph and Metalzoa, with the new relic Metal Coating. Red-Eyes Black Metal Dragon joins his pack. Cards that summon themselves from the Deck now light up the Deck: tap it to summon them.

8 new languages

Play in German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean or Chinese, cards included. Tap the flag on the title screen to switch.

New & Changes

  • Pegasus starts with Dark-Eyes Illusionist instead of Thousand-Eyes Idol.
  • Boss rerolls in loop 1 never bring back a curse from the previous roll.
  • Card info shows monster subtypes, like Spirit · Effect.
  • Main menu cards use a new card-style font.
  • Copycat & Dueling Engine: no more Edit deck button, and Bounty List shows in the shop.
  • The Guardian shop boost now includes the Guardian equip Spells.

Smarter CPU

  • Summons a blocker when Light of Intervention stops it Setting.
  • Keeps Kuriboh in hand instead of summoning it.
  • No longer adds Waboku after its Enemy Controller already stopped the attack.

Fixes

  • Tag: changing the deck editor's sort no longer opens solo screens, the trade list keeps its place, hand monsters show their ATK on the field, and the "?" reveal works.
  • Tag: a failed direct connection now tries again later.
  • Relic Seal: the hand size limit shown now ignores sealed relics.
