**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.35**

## Randomized cards
Brand-new cards made on the spot: names, art, ATK/DEF, Types and effects are rolled at random from the cards in the game, and every one is checked so it works in a Duel. A rainbow frame marks them. They last for the run they're found in (solo runs only).
- **Random Pack:** 9 randomized cards. Can take a pack slot in about 1 in 20 shops.
- **Absolute Random** (100): open a free Random Pack after each victory.
- **Copycat: Randomizer** loadout: a whole run of randomized cards, 4 Random Packs to start and a fully randomized shop.

## Nine new relics (suggested on Discord)
- **Magician's Bond** (90): once per turn, if you Normal Summon a "Dark Magician" monster, add 1 card that mentions "Dark Magician" from your Deck to your hand.
- **Master and Apprentice** (100): once per turn, if a "Dark Magician" monster you control is destroyed, Special Summon 1 "Dark Magician" monster from your hand.
- **Black Chaos Rite** (110): once per Duel, Tribute 1 "Dark Magician" you control; Special Summon "Dark Magician of Chaos" from your hand or Deck.
- **Blue-Eyes Radiance** (85): once per turn, send 3 "Blue-Eyes White Dragon" from hand/field to the GY; Special Summon "Blue-Eyes Shining Dragon" from your hand or Deck.
- **Twin Eyes** (150): once per Duel, during your first Standby Phase, Special Summon 1 "Blue-Eyes" or "Red-Eyes" monster from your Deck.
- **Dragon Blood Fusion** (100): once per turn, banish 1 "Blue-Eyes" or "Red-Eyes" monster you control; Special Summon a Fusion Monster that lists it as material.
- **Dragon Lord's Decree** (90): once per Duel, discard 2 Dragon monsters; Special Summon 1 Level 7 or higher Dragon monster from your hand.
- **Titan's Charge** (100): once per turn, when your Level 7 or higher monster destroys a monster by battle, it can attack again in a row.
- **Poison of the Old Man** (cursed): once per turn, during your End Phase, toss a coin. Heads: gain 500 LP. Tails: lose 500 LP.

## New
- **Pick cards where they are:** tributes, materials and discards from your hand glow and are tapped right in your hand, like monsters on the field. A window only opens when some choices are elsewhere (GY, Deck, banished), and it lists every choice with a badge saying where it is.
- **See the next boss early:** tap the VICTORIES strip to see your next boss and its curses. Loop 13 is now the final loop.
- **Draft Duels:** relic offers are weighted by rarity, fused relics are no longer offered, and the host can tick "No relics" for a packs-only match.
- **PC:** hold R for 3 seconds to restart the run with the same duelist, loadout and level, until your first Duel ends.

## Changes
- Packs tear open. UI Scale setting (Auto is bigger on 4K screens).
- Destroyersaurus and Jurassic World join Rex Raptor's draft pack and Savage Wilds.
- Tag: both players starting with the same relic gives the team +50 coins for the extra copy.
- Controller: the D-pad stays in a card's Summon / Set / Inspect menu and B / Circle closes it; Xbox pads show Xbox buttons; the button guide can be hidden (Settings › Gameplay).
- Import a save file from the browser version into the app (Android file picker fixed).
- "Choose a card" shows printed ATK/DEF with the On the field stats under it. Deck editor ATK/DEF scales with the card size. Too many copies of a card: the message names the card. Ritual Scar shows its current ATK change. Favourite star on guaranteed packs. Tap outside a window to close it. Space confirms a selection.
