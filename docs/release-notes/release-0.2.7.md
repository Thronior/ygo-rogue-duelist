**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.7**

**New relics** (thanks Muniqui for the ideas!)
- **Battle Proficiency** (110): your opponent takes 50% more battle damage.
- **Burn Proficiency** (100): effect damage you inflict on your opponent is increased by 50%.
- **Straw Doll** (130): once per opponent turn, the first battle damage you would take becomes 0.
- **Almighty** (300, very rare): your monsters' ATK/DEF can't be reduced, even by your own cursed relics and boss curses.
- **Deck Thinning** (100): your Main Deck minimum is 10 cards lower (min. 20).
- **Relic Picker** (100): choose 1 of 3 relics you don't own (1 reroll). Used up when picked.
- **Card Picker** (200): choose any card in the game for your card pool. Used up when picked.
- **Name Changer** (150): choose an archetype; your monsters also count as it.
- **Anime Rule #1** (55): Normal Summon monsters in face-up Defense Position.
- **Mind Reader** (80): at your Standby Phase, look at 1 of your opponent's Set cards. It stays Set.

**New cursed relics**
- **Defense Proficiency**: halve all damage you take, but your battle damage is halved too.
- **Singleton Pact**: your Main Deck minimum is always 20, but your Deck can hold only 1 copy of each card.

**Changes**
- **Collector's Burden** now raises your Main Deck minimum by 10. It's only offered from the second boss on, where that's still 50, and it now stacks with Deck Thinning.
- The deck editor shows the right minimum with Collector's Burden.
- The CPU accounts for the new damage relics and no longer wastes stat-down cards into Almighty.
- Relic Picker, Card Picker, Name Changer and Singleton Pact aren't offered in Tag Duels.
