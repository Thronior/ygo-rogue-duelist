*YGO Rogue Duelist v0.1.88*

*New Relics*

Good Spirits | 80 coins | Spirit monsters you control do not return to the hand at the end of the turn.
Dark Scorpion Squad | 90 coins | Dark Scorpion monsters gain 200 ATK for each face-up Dark Scorpion on the field.
Gravekeeper's Union | 90 coins | Gravekeeper monsters gain 200 ATK for each face-up Gravekeeper on the field.
Amazoness Hunters | 90 coins | Amazoness monsters gain 200 ATK for each face-up Amazoness on the field.

Level One | 90 coins | Add a random Level 1–4 LV monster from your Deck to your starting hand.
LEVEL UP | 90 coins | Add 1 "Level Up!" to your starting hand.

*Relic Changes*
Level Bonus now gives LV monsters 400 ATK. Razor Ledger checks the current minimum Deck size (20, 30, or 40). Relic prices were reduced by 10 coins as requested, and descriptions were corrected.

*Shop and Packs*
Relics can now be sold for half their original price. Owning an attribute-changing relic lowers the appearance chance of other attribute-changing relics and elemental orbs. Booster pack descriptions now list archetypes when present.

*Characters and Fixes*
Ryou Bakura's new variant, *Fortune's Favorite*, starts with a random archetype-shop relic. Level One only selects LV monsters. Solomon's Level -1 shop layout is corrected, and menu artwork and asset retries are more reliable.
