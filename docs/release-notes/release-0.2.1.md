**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.1**

**Duel & CPU**
- The CPU now weighs Blind Destruction and Needle Wall by their expected board impact, uses Des Counterblow against direct attacks, and evaluates Dangerous Machine Type-6 and Dice Re-Roll by likely value.
- Improved use of Dark Core and Canyon, and fixed Sand Moth so it can revive after battle destruction.
- Improved CPU decisions for Rescue Cat, Monster Reincarnation, Infinite Cards, Spell Absorption, Ra and Juragedo, King of the Swamp, Dragon's Bead, Kunai with Chain, and De-Fusion. The CPU preserves useful God-card recovery lines and favors Ra boosts when they create a safe battle advantage.

**Opponents & Decks**
- Added T2 Archfiend for Darkbishop Archfiend, T2 Blue-Eyes 2 for Kisara, T1 and T2 Seeker decks, and T1 and T2 Siegfried decks.
- Added Arcana Knight Joker to Siegfried's T3 Extra Deck.

**Packs**
- Added a Limited Edition Collection combining the 56 unique cards from Japanese Limited Edition packs 1–8, using the Limited Edition 8 pack artwork.
- Added Arcana Knight Joker to character packs containing any of its materials.
