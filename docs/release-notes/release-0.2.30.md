**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.30**

## Card sleeves
A new **Cosmetics** menu with 90 card sleeves. Clear Challenge Level 6 with a duelist to earn their rainbow sleeve, and unlock 54 card-art sleeves with feats like Ritual Summons, banishing and wins across runs. Your sleeve shows on your face-down cards and Deck, and in Tag your partner's sleeve shows too.

## Pixel Art
A 7th Duel Look: a pixel font, stepped pixel frames, and pixelated cards, art, portraits and backgrounds. Pick it in Cosmetics, or switch to it mid-duel.

## Eight new relics
Suggested on Discord:
- **Typhoon** (135): during your End Phase, destroy 1 of your Spells/Traps to destroy 1 of your opponent's.
- **Machine Duplication** (110): during your End Phase, a Clone Token with half the ATK/DEF of a Machine you control.
- **Omni Banishment** (150): during your Standby Phase, banish 1 card from your Deck.
- **Ghost Destruction** (80): when your monster deals battle damage, banish a monster from either GY.
- **Spell Provider** (60): during your Standby Phase, place 1 Spell Counter on a card that can hold one.
- **Dimensional Rift** (300, rare): every card that would go to the GY is banished instead.
- **Anime Rule #3** (cursed): Level 5+ monsters need no Tribute, but your monsters lose 1000 ATK/DEF without a face-up Field Spell.
- **Cursed Restock** (cursed): 2 free rerolls in every shop, but only one purchase per stock.

## Cooler phones
With an FPS Cap set, a bug since v0.2.28 kept multiplying the animation loop, so phones ran hot and choppy. Fixed: 24 → 58 frames per second in our test, with less CPU.

## Champion Trophy
After each boss fight you can pick a second cursed relic (was +40 coins). Each extra Trophy adds another pick.

## New opponent
**Duke Devlin** joins as a Tier 3 opponent with a coin-toss deck: Second Coin Toss, Gambler of Legend, Cup of Ace and Jirai Gumo, with new CPU rules for all of them.

## New & Changes
- **Mokuba Kaiba (Tier 1)** and **Crump (Tier 3)** get new decks from the community.
- **Mako Tsunami's pack** adds Orca Mega-Fortress of Darkness.
- **Yami Yugi's pack:** 15 weaker cards removed; the Exodia pieces are rarer.
- **Enchanted Javelin** gets a dev note: it only works on your opponent's attacks.
- **Coin Reward Pop-ups** switch in Settings › Effects.

## Smarter CPU
- **Second Coin Toss** is now played, and only redoes a bad toss.
- **Fairy Box:** the CPU stops paying 500 LP a turn when it doesn't need it.

## Fixes
- **Riryoku** and other cards now say what you're picking, e.g. "Select the monster to halve its ATK".
- **Magic Mirror:** a copied stat relic no longer boosts your cards in the shop.
- **Shop categories:** cards that weaken a Type or Attribute (like Star Boy for FIRE) are no longer offered as its support.
- **Anime Rule #2** only appears as a cursed relic, no longer in the shop.
- **iPhone:** "Progress could not be saved" and "Duel could not start" errors now retry instead of failing.
