**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.36**

## Important fixes
- **Start Looping** crashed from loop 2 on in 0.2.35, so no run could reach loop 3. Fixed.
- In German, opening **Settings › Display** (or picking German there) froze the game, in the Windows app and in browsers. Fixed.
- **Tag:** two players on the same version were told to update if one had opened a Random Pack in solo. Fixed, and a real version mismatch now says whose game is out of date.

## Random Packs go online
- **Tag:** the shop can stock a Random Pack (about 1 in 20 shops), and Absolute Random can be found. Both players see the same cards.
- **Draft Duels:** the Random Pack joins the Draft Screen rotation, next to the character packs.
- **Clearer effects:** randomized cards roll simpler, tidier effects: fewer costs and actions per effect, and amounts closer to real cards.
- In solo shops the Random Pack only takes the second booster slot, never your character pack. Randomized cards are banned in Ultimate Collector: they stay behind when you import a run.

## New
- **Draft Duels second loadouts:** pick a character's second loadout and start with its packs and signature cards (unlocked as in solo: Challenge LVL 2 or LVL -1). Copycat's Randomizer starts with 4 Random Packs.
- **Mark your cards** (solo and Tag deck editors): tap a card's corner to cycle ☆ → ★ → ✕. ✕ cards are greyed out and sorted last, and your marks help the shop pick cards for your deck.
- **Card window:** Add to deck and Remove from deck buttons; tap a card's Type, Attribute, Level or a card name in its text to search for it.
- **Main menu:** a bell next to Discord shows every patch notes sheet, and the newest one pops up once after an update. A ✕ Close button at the top right closes the game (Windows app and Android).
- A locked duelist's Start button shows how to unlock them.

## Changes
- The Relics window shows your run's challenge level and what it does.
- Level search: Levels 1-4, 5-6, 7-8, 9-12. Level sort puts the highest ATK first within a Level.
- Huge collections (9,000+ cards) load fast in the deck editor.
- More coin tiers: Damage, Effect damage and LP recovered up to 32,000, Largest hit up to 10,000.
- Your Set monsters show their face-up ATK/DEF with relic bonuses. The cursed relic screen shows your LP and coins.
- Twin Eyes now summons from your hand or Deck. Blast with Chain asks in the Damage Step on Chain sometimes.
- Character portrait sizes are normalized. The main menu tells you when cloud save is paused or couldn't upload.

## Fixes
- The hidden relicless fight could get a run stuck on loading.
- New fused relics showed as ??? until the next duel.
- ATK forecasts respect DNA Surgery's declared Type. Gate Calling asks with the right question.
- Blood Toll is no longer offered in Tag (no LP payments there).
- Collection sorting keeps the default order for ties; ATK/DEF sorts put Spells before Traps.
