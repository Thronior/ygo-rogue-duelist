**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.4**

**New**
- Shareable end-of-run card: your run as a character sheet with stats, relics, cause of defeat and duel record. Losses get cracked stone, wins a frame in your Challenge Level's colour (gold glitter at LVL 5, prismatic foil at LVL 6). **Copy image** puts it on your clipboard for Discord, or saves it where copying isn't supported.
- Pre-errata cards: 22 cards now play their original print, incl. Sangan and Witch of the Black Forest (no once per turn), Ring of Destruction (any face-up monster, damages both players), Brain Control (can take Fusion/Ritual monsters), Sinister Serpent, Dark Magician of Chaos, Rescue Cat, Chaos Emperor Dragon, Imperial Order and Crush Card Virus.

**Packs & characters**
- Removed the WC2004 booster packs except the "all X cards" ones (all Spells, Traps, Effect Monsters, Monsters). Runs that held a removed pack get the all-Monsters pack instead.
- Paradox Brothers' second variant is now **Labyrinth Giants**: Tribute Free relic, Guardian Angel Joan, Invader of Darkness and Black Tyranno, with Invasion of Chaos and Dark Revelation 1.
- Pegasus starts with Duelist Legacy Volume 1 instead of the Relinquished pack.
- Mako Tsunami's T1 deck runs Negate Attack instead of Tornado Wall.

**Fixes**
- Monsters in your hand show the stats they'll have on the field with your relics and Field Spells.
- The position prompt no longer stays on screen during summon animations.
- Graveyard cards with usable effects get their yellow border again.
- Fixed Rush Duel or "SAMPLE" artwork on Grand Tiki Elder, Fairy Dragon, Aitsu, Melchid the Four-Face Beast, Yaranzo, Shovel Crusher, Kaiser Dragon and Gilford the Lightning.
- CPU Exodia decks keep drawing for missing pieces near the end of their deck.

**Online**
- Ultimate Collector and Draft Duel rooms only pair players on the same game version, so update before playing a friend.
