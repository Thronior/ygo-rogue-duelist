**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.15**

**Online**
- New netcode for Tag, Draft Duels and Ultimate Collector, built for long-distance and unstable connections.
- Your own taps now appear instantly, even on high ping.
- Dropped connections reconnect on their own, and a short drop no longer costs you timer.
- New ping dot, plus clear waiting and lost-connection messages.
- Update both players before playing together.

**New**
- Relics: Token Regiment (90 coins, Tokens gain 750 ATK), Token Resonance (100 coins, Tokens gain double boosts) and Token Martyrs (90 coins, 500 damage per Token destroyed).
- Cursed relic Ominous Clock: 600 damage each opponent Standby Phase, down 100 every 10 turns, and healing them below 0.
- The Fusion Deck glows when a monster can be Special Summoned from it.
- Dark Paladin summon animation, and Man i forgor joins the fallen Ultimate Collectors.

**Changes**
- CPU: plays over 20 new combos across its decks, and attacks before Setting with Muka Muka and Slifer.
- Ritual Dagger now adds a Ritual card to every shop, and is offered less often.
- Reversed Remedy also turns LP costs into LP gain, and is rarer.
- Dark Scorpion Squad counts Don Zaloog.
- Marik Ishtar now starts with Burn Proficiency.
- Yami and Yugi's pack gains 4 Fusion substitutes, plus Metamorphosis as a rare.
- Perfect has a clean new chime, and finisher lines have more echo.

**Fixes**
- Fixed the "setMatch" error that froze duels with Necrovalley active.
- Stray Lambs, Scapegoat and similar cards can't be wasted under Berserker's Pact.
- Face-down cards no longer offer Inspect after Cyber Jar.
- Coin tosses in Tag no longer show the result early or stall.
- Where to Find now scrolls, and holding cards no longer highlights text.
- CPU: fixed Serket, Tribe-Infecting Virus, Stealth Bird, Cyber Raider with Amplifier, Rocket Warrior and Enemy Controller on Spirit Reaper.
