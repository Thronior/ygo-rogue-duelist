**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.21**

## New relics
- **Field of Attraction:** each time you Normal Summon a Magnet Warrior, add a Magnet Warrior with a different name from your Deck to your hand.
- **Deep Pockets:** your hand size limit is increased by 3.
- **More Relic Fusions:** new fusions to discover, cursed relics included.

## Deck balancing
36 opponent decks were rebalanced so every duelist is a fair fight for their tier.
- **Tier 1:** Cecelia Pegasus, Darkbishop Archfiend, Joey Wheeler, Odion, Roland, Simon, Tea Gardner, Weevil Underwood, Yugi Muto
- **Tier 2:** Alister, Amazoness Paladin, Dark Magician, Darkbishop Archfiend, Espa Roba, Ishizu Ishtar, Kemo, Lumis and Umbra, Mahad, Noah Kaiba, Rex Raptor, Simon, Yami Bakura, Yugi Muto
- **Tier 3:** Alister, Crump, Dark Magician, Johnson, Mahad, Marik Ishtar, Sid, Strings, Tea Gardner, The Masked Beast, Thestalos, Valon, Yami Bakura

## New & Changes
- **Paradox Brothers:** new Magnet Warriors loadout with Field of Attraction. The Standard loadout starts with Tribute Free. Their pack gained the Magnet Warriors, Valkyrion, Wasteland and Gaia Power.
- **Yami Yugi** starts with Dark Magician Girl instead of Thousand Knives.
- **Stats menu:** Device, Global and Character Stats have their own menu option.
- **Deck builder:** inspecting a card shows the Where to Find tab.
- **Tag run card:** Tag runs get their own run card, on the end screen and in Run History.
- **Ring of Destruction** is back to its post-errata version.
- **Chain Sometimes:** A Feint Plan only asks during your opponent's Battle Phase.
- **Duel Looks:** usable phase buttons glow, the rest are greyed out.
- **Duelist select:** more compact in portrait.

## Smarter CPU
- Half or Nothing, Mask of Weakness and Creature Swap are only used when they help.
- Last Will isn't used after a negated summon, and Formation Union is no longer spammed.
- Never pays an LP cost that would make it lose.
- Tributes after attacking, and plays field spells and revival walls better.
- Slate Warrior is summoned face-up when it can attack.
- 0-ATK walls like Labyrinth Wall are now Tribute Set.
- Waboku is chained to save its targets or against field wipes.
- Compulsory Evacuation Device, Magical Dimension and Soul of Purity and Light are used better.
- Gravity Bind and Messenger of Peace go up when you out-muscle the CPU.
- Solemn Wishes stacks, and Fire Princess is shown when safe.

## Fixes
- Stand Tall no longer causes heavy lag with Magic Mirror, two copies, or Token Resonance.
- The CPU reads stats from the new fused relics correctly.
