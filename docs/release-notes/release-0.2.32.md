**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.32**

## Shops follow your Deck
Relic and card odds now read the Deck you play, not your whole collection.
- Specific themes pull hardest: an all-Amazoness Deck sees Amazoness relics far more often, ahead of broad Warrior or EARTH ones.
- General stock singles follow the same rules.
- Relics and cards for themes your Deck lacks show up less.
- Relics you pass on appear less in the next two stocks.
- Early shops offer fewer relics you can't afford yet.
- Tag: both players' Decks shape the shared shop.

## Relic locks
Tap the lock on a relic's art to keep it through rerolls and into the next shop, until you buy or unlock it. Works in Tag too.

## Eight new relic fusions
Collect the right relics and they fuse into one stronger relic. Can you find the recipes?
- **Gadget Gadget:** "Gadget" monsters you control gain 500 ATK. Each time you Normal Summon a "Gadget" monster, Special Summon 2 "Gadget" monsters with different names from your Deck, then add 1 "Stronghold the Moving Fortress" from your Deck to your hand.
- **Spell Counter Engine:** During your Standby Phase, place up to 2 Spell Counters on cards you control that can hold them. Monsters you control gain 200 ATK for each Spell Counter on your field.
- **Level Up Chain:** Start each Duel with a random Level 1–4 LV monster from your Deck and a copy of "Level Up!" in your hand. Your LV monsters gain 500 ATK and are unaffected by monster effects.
- **Terrorking Magnus:** Whenever you pay LP for an Archfiend card, recover twice the amount paid. Each Archfiend monster you control gains 400 ATK for each Archfiend monster you control, including itself.
- **Spirit Sanctuary:** Spirit monsters you control gain 500 ATK and may stay on the field during the End Phase. You can Tribute Summon Spirit monsters by banishing monsters from your GY in place of Tributes. Whenever a monster returns from the field to your hand, gain 500 LP.
- **Living Traps:** Trap Monsters you control gain 600 ATK/DEF and cannot be destroyed by battle. The first time each Duel you Special Summon a Trap Monster, you may Set a different Trap Monster directly from your Deck.
- **Scorched Hourglass:** At the start of your Standby Phase, inflict 500 damage to your opponent. Whenever you inflict effect damage to your opponent from another source, inflict 500 additional damage. This bonus applies once per originating damage event. Damage caused by this relic cannot trigger the bonus.
- **Guardian’s Armory:** Start each Duel with a random Guardian monster and its corresponding Equip Spell in your hand. Equipped monsters you control gain 500 ATK. A Guardian monster you control cannot be targeted or destroyed by card effects while its corresponding Equip Spell is face-up on the field.

## New relics & curses
- **Armory Aid** (70): start each Duel with a random Equip Spell from your Deck in hand.
- **Spiritual Retribution** (70): once per Duel, Tribute Summon a Spirit by banishing monsters from your GY.
- **Disturbed Sleep** (80): each End Phase, face-down Defense monsters flip to face-up Attack.
- **Gadget Call** (90): a Gadget that would add another Gadget to your hand Special Summons it instead.
- **Gadget Stronghold** (70): start with Stronghold the Moving Fortress in hand.
- **Good Spirits** (reworked): choose for each Spirit whether it returns to the hand.
- **Vampiric Contact** (cursed): gain 500 LP per monster you destroy by battle; lose LP equal to the ATK of yours destroyed.
- **Rain of Mercy** (curse): your opponent gains 400 LP at the start of their Standby Phase.

## Changes
- **Tag loops:** play the final 1v1 to unlock the next loop. Win or lose, the team keeps everything; skipping ends the run.
- **Bandit Keith's pack** adds Red, Green and Yellow Gadget and Gear Golem the Moving Fortress.
- **Flip effects filter** now includes "when Flip Summoned" cards like Guardian Sphinx.
- **Deck-out wins** keep at least a third of the duel's coins despite the slow duel penalty.
- **Duke Devlin** now unlocks by rolling 10 dice and tossing 10 coins.
- **Draft Duels deck editor** gets sorting, Deck status, split view and ATK/DEF like solo.
- **Firefox:** a notice explains Compatibility Mode and suggests other browsers.

## Fixes
- **Android:** holding a card no longer closes its window right away (also Draft relics and packs).
- **Some phones:** the deck editor no longer seems stuck hovering one card.
- **Right-clicking a pack** opens it again; right-click only closes windows.
- **CPU:** no more endless Sinister Serpent loop under Dark Ruler Ha Des.
