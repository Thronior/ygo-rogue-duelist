**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.14**

**New**
- Eight new booster packs in the shop's random slots and as opponent rewards, including Fortune's Gambit and Stinkers.
- Keyboard shortcuts on web: Space for next phase, Enter to confirm, C for chain prompts, L for the duel log.
- Settings → Display: Text Size and Reduce Motion.
- Android: optional vibration on big hits and chain prompts.
- Replays: see each duel's relics and curses, and flag behaviour from the replay.

**Relics**
- Weighted Coins (100 coins): choose heads or tails when you get it. Your coins are twice as likely to land on that face.
- Weighted Dice (100 coins): choose a number from 1 to 6 when you get it. Your dice are three times as likely to land on it.
- Granite Heart (70 coins): your Rock monsters gain 300 ATK.
- Restless Stone (120 coins): your Rock monsters can change battle position the turn they are Summoned or Set.
- Relic offers now lean toward cards in your collection.
- Berserker's Pact: your monsters no longer have to attack. Instead, you cannot Set monsters or Special Summon them in Defense Position.
- Selling your last DNA Rewrite clears its Type, so buying it again asks for a new one.

**Changes**
- Duke Devlin's pack grew to 142 cards, with better odds of finding gambling cards.
- Theinen the Great Sphinx joins Anubis's signature cards.
- Duel log: turn dividers, card thumbnails and colours for each side.
- Threatening Roar can now prompt you when the opponent ends their Main Phase on Chain Sometimes.
- Necrovalley is now a common in Odion's draft pack.

**Fixes**
- Android update prompts now appear before the game loads.
- Spell and Trap cards no longer all read "Continuous" during a duel.
- Deck editor stats now include Level 6 curses.
- CPU: no longer aims Tribute to the Doomed or Raigeki Break at monsters that cannot be targeted, or moves Power Stone counters with no valid monster.
- CPU: better Dust Tornado, Rescue Cat, Dragon's Rage and Mirror of Yata decisions.
