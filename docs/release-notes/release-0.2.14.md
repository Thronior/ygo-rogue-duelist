**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.14**

**New**
- Eight new booster packs in the shop's random slots and as opponent rewards, including Fortune's Gambit, Knights and Magi, and Stinkers.
- Relics: Weighted Coins, Weighted Dice, and Restless Stone. Granite Heart now works too.
- Relic offers now lean toward cards in your collection.
- Keyboard shortcuts on web: Space for next phase, Enter to confirm, C for chain prompts, L for the duel log.
- Settings → Display: Text Size and Reduce Motion.
- Android: optional vibration on big hits and chain prompts.
- Replays: see each duel's relics and curses, and flag behaviour from the replay.

**Changes**
- Duke Devlin's pack grew to 142 cards, with more gambling cards and better odds of finding them.
- Theinen the Great Sphinx joins Anubis's signature cards.
- Duel log: turn dividers, card thumbnails and clearer colours for each side.
- Berserker's Pact: you cannot Set monsters, and Special Summons land in Attack Position.
- Threatening Roar can now prompt you when the opponent ends their Main Phase on Chain Sometimes.
- Necrovalley is now a common in Odion's draft pack.

**Fixes**
- Android update prompts now appear before the game loads.
- Spell and Trap cards no longer all read "Continuous" during a duel.
- Deck editor stats now include Level 6 curses.
- Selling your last DNA Rewrite clears its Type, so buying it again asks for a new one.
- Duel log wording fixed ("Opponent takes 300 LP").
- CPU: no longer targets monsters that cannot be targeted with Tribute to the Doomed or Raigeki Break, or moves Power Stone counters with no valid monster.
- CPU: better Dust Tornado, Rescue Cat, Dragon's Rage and Mirror of Yata decisions.
