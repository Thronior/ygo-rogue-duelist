**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.29**

## Tutorial duel
A short guided duel against Solomon Muto teaches the phases, the card types and the card window, then walks you through every play. Find it in Settings › About and Tutorial. New players are asked once when they start their first run. Nothing is saved.

## Six new relics
Suggested on Discord, now in the shop:
- **Stars Aligned** (175): during your End Phase, if every monster in your GY and on your field has the same Level, Special Summon 1 monster of that Level from your hand.
- **Illusionary Tribute** (150): once per Duel, Tribute your Spells and Traps for a Tribute Summon.
- **Tactical Espionage** (100): when you Normal Summon, no Trap Cards can be activated.
- **Overkill Bounty** (90): +20 coins for every 1000 damage your final blow deals past 0 LP.
- **Parting Gift** (80): once per turn, draw 1 when an opponent's effect sends your monster to the GY.
- **Anime Rule #2** (cursed): destroying a monster by effect burns for half its ATK, but your monsters can't attack directly.

## New relic window
Your run relics are now an icon grid: tap one to see its card. Relics in the shop, in duels and in Collection get the same new look. Between duels, Level 6 permanent curses are listed too, and the opponent screen has a Relics button.

## Records
A new Records tab in Run History: the highest level cleared for every duelist, their runs, wins, win rate and wins per level.

## Smoother animations
With an FPS Cap set, frames are now evenly spaced, so card drags, reward spins and pack openings no longer stutter. Ultra Rare pulls no longer hitch.

## New & Changes
- **Deck list in duels:** tap your Deck › Inspect to see the cards left, sorted.
- **Joey (Armed Duelist):** signature cards are now Gearfried the Iron Knight, Release Restraint and Gearfried the Swordmaster.
- **Duke Devlin** starts with Let's Go Gambling (was Dice Jar).
- **Kaiba's pack:** the XYZ fusions are less rare, and XZ and YZ are in the Union shop category.
- **Cloud save:** backs up the moment you win Duel 9, even if you keep looping, and uses far fewer server requests.
- **VICTORY** banner always stays up long enough to read.

## Smarter CPU
- **Tower of Babel:** never plays the 4th Spell itself, and chains so yours becomes the 4th.
- **Ordeal of a Traveler:** remembers the cards it revealed and guesses from them.
- **Dark Spirit of the Silent** waits for your strongest attacker; **Reliable Guardian** only on a face-up defender; **Enemy Controller** isn't wasted when Sakuretsu Armor already stops the attack.

## Balance
- **Conscription Drum:** now a permanent Double Summon: 2 Normal Summons or Sets per turn, in any order, with no prompt.
- **Faulty Printer:** misprint chance 15% → 20%.
- **Premature Triumph, Thin Ranks:** your opening hand can now go down to 0 cards.

## Fixes
- **Limiter Removal:** Chain Sometimes only asks during attacks, the Damage Step or a chain.
- **Translations:** cursed relic benefits and drawbacks are translated, and deck sorting works in every language.
- **Low-LP glow** lines up with the LP bar again on phones.
- **Duel loading screen** shows its tip instead of raw code.
- **"Not enough gold"** messages no longer stack up or linger.
