# Update 0.2.18

## New
- **Deck Assembler:** a second Dueling Engine loadout. Starts with a random Structure Deck, builds his own deck, and each win offers a whole starter or Structure Deck for his pool.
- **Second loadouts in Tag Duels:** both players can pick their unlocked second loadout.
- **Coins:** capped at 999,999, and the counter shows ∞ at the cap.

## Cursed relics
- **Fusion Fever:** Fusions gain 1000 ATK and piercing. You can't Tribute Summon or Set.
- **Ritual Scar:** your monsters lose 500 ATK, then gain 100 ATK per Ritual Summon this run.
- **Crowded Crypt:** drawback is now hand size limit -4.
- **Golden Grave:** 6 coins per GY monster (max. 90), monsters lose 400 DEF.
- **Loaded Purse:** 60 coins, opponent starts with 4000 extra LP.
- **Empty-Hand Pact:** hand size limit reduced by 2, and it stacks.
- **Champion's Burden:** removed.

## Changes
- Jade Pendant costs 80. Restless Stone is once per turn. LEVEL UP no longer asks you to pick.
- Duke Devlin starts with Dice Jar.
- Reversed Remedy and Effect Reverser now cancel out.
- Starter and Structure Decks are in the Collection's Packs tab. All 22 can appear in the shop, with 5 duplicates removed.
- Selection pop-ups show where each card is.
- Relics that ask for input show their art first.
- Exxod, Master of The Guard gets a summon animation.
- Smaller battle button on phones. New Token Resonance art.

## CPU
- Understands attack locks like Magician's Valkyria.
- Sangan won't attack into Baou for a search it can't get.
- Lava and Swamp Battleguard count each other's bonus.
- Picks Attack Position when it wins that fight.

## Fixes
- Anime Rule #1: face-down Special Summons stay face-down.
- Tag: fixed a softlock after a solo duel, and players no longer stay stuck on "Run paused" after a silent disconnect.
- Saves above the old coin limit open again.
