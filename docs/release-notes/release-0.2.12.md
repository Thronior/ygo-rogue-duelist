**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.12**

**New**
- Duel replays: in Collection → Run History, expand a run to see a portrait of every opponent you faced. Tap one to watch that duel again, with play/pause, step, step back and speed controls. Your last 100 duels are kept; older replays are deleted automatically.
- Beating Challenge Level 6 now gives a rolling rainbow look on the character select roster, the LVL 6 badge and the win share card.
- The webapp checks for updates every time it starts and updates itself, so a normal refresh is enough (no more Ctrl+F5). Skipped when you are offline.

**Changes**
- Challenge Levels 5 and 6 swapped: Level 5 now gives opponents a combat relic, and curses only persist from Level 6.
- Straw Doll now costs 250 coins (was 130).

**Fixes**
- Hand stats ("On the field") are now accurate: they no longer count Equip Spells still in your hand, and they include monsters' own effects (Mudora, Amazoness Tiger, Muka Muka, Dark Magician Girl, Star Boy…), Necrovalley, Eclipse's DEF and Graveyard counts after a Tribute Summon.
- Phoenix Feather is only used up when it actually saves you, not whenever you win at 1,000 LP or less.
- Pandemonium no longer offers an Archfiend search when it only waived an Archfiend's Standby Phase cost.
- CPU: no longer gets stuck repeatedly activating a negated effect (e.g. Sinister Serpent).
- CPU: Little Winguard keeps a safe Defense Position instead of always switching in the End Phase.
