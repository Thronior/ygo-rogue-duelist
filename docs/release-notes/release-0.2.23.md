**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.23**

## Wide-screen deck editor
On wide screens the deck editor splits in two: your Deck on the left and your Collection on the right. Tap a card to move it across; both sides keep their place. Works in solo and Tag.

## New relic: Corpse Ritual
When you Ritual Summon, you can banish monsters from your GY as material instead of Tributing them from your hand or field.

## New setting: Auto-Placement
Cards you summon, Set or activate go to a zone automatically, picked the way the opponent picks. Targets and Tributes stay your choice.

## New Tier 3 decks
Mai Valentine, Yami Bakura, Rex Raptor, Bonz, Valon, Mahad, Darkbishop Archfiend and Zorc Necrophades bring new Tier 3 decks.

## New & Changes
- **Duel Looks** now restyle buttons and dropdowns across the game, and button text is bold.
- **Shop:** buttons follow your Duel Look, and Next Duel is a crossed-swords button.
- **Deck Card Size:** Auto is now a switch; turn it off to set the size yourself.
- **Settings:** Saved Data moved into the Devices & Apps tab.

## Smarter CPU
- Now plays Blast with Chain, Metalmorph and Snipe Hunter.
- Uses the effects of Orca Mega-Fortress of Darkness, Robbin' Zombie and Vampire Genesis.
- Plays Ultra Evolution Pill, and Soul Release with Giant Rex.
- Summons Legion the Fiend Jester, then Tributes it for an extra Spellcaster.
- Juragedo waits in hand to Special Summon itself during an attack.
- Reaper on the Nightmare is fused, attacks, and is never Tributed.
- Smarter equips: none on borrowed monsters, and no stacking with no attack left.
- Magical Dimension, Tribe-Infecting Virus and Abyss Soldier are used at better moments.

## Fixes
- Bosses with Ritual Spells no longer get No Specials Allowed below LVL 2, so they can Ritual Summon.
- The CPU no longer Normal Summons Nuvia the Wicked or Boar Soldier, or equips its own Spirit Reaper.
