**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.13**

**New**
- Boss summons: 30 more boss monsters now have a summon animation (52 in total), including Blue-Eyes White Dragon, Red-Eyes Black Dragon and Dark Magician.
- Boss voice lines: all 52 announce their name during their summon animation.
- Settings → Effects: turn individual effects on or off, including monster flames, screen shake, finishing blow banners, the low LP warning and more.
- Settings → Sound: new separate sliders for Voice Lines and Menu Sounds. They start at your current Sound Effects level.
- Deck editor: new Extra Deck tab next to the Deck tab, showing every Extra Deck card you own.

**Changes**
- Bandit Keith's Fusion Arsenal now starts with Gatling Dragon.
- The Paradox Brothers' standard version now starts with Gate Guardian.
- Shared Hex has been removed. Saved runs that hold it keep working; the relic is simply removed.

**Fixes**
- The yellow activation frame now shows on monsters with flames behind them.
- In the in-duel settings, the floating Chain button no longer covers the toggles.
