**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.8**

**Duel effects**
- The screen edge reddens as your LP drains, strong monsters burn, big hits and summons land harder, and portraits react to hits and heals.
- Finishers: exact lethal is PERFECT, and every 1,000 overkill earns a bigger word (BRUTAL, SAVAGE, GODLIKE…) with a voice line.
- Your hand shakes over the hand limit, plus clearer deck-out warnings and chain build-ups. Performance Mode keeps it calm.

**Shop**
- The shopkeeper talks: welcomes, reactions to what you buy, rerolls and pokes. Poking him works in every shop again.
- Unaffordable items dim, purchases get a SOLD stamp, rerolls spin, and the coin payout is a window over the shop.

**New**
- Run card shows only the curses of your final duel.
- Archetype relics only show up once your run has a card of that archetype. Name Changer's pick counts.
- Relics bar in portrait duels: curses sit in the relic row at relic size, on one shorter row. With 5+, the next one peeks out.

**CPU** (from 1,216 of your replays)
- Uses Fissure, Raigeki and other destruction on battle-proof walls like Marshmallon and Spirit Reaper.
- Plans lethal through your blockers: it summons or switches to Attack the monster that finishes you, and uses removal, revival, Lightning Vortex, Change of Heart and Royal Decree to open the way.
- Ring of Destruction can finish you (the game uses the original card).
- Casts Swords of Revealing Light sooner, Heavy Storm before its own Snatch Steal, and actually calls Fairy Box's coin toss.
- Stops attacking flipped-down monsters whose relic or Field Spell DEF beats its attacker.
- No longer counts monsters that already attacked.
- Ceasefire counts its own set monsters; no God is tributed for another God.

**Fixes**
- Hand "On the field" stats now really include every stat relic: Jester's Coin, Kindred Bond, Overcharge, Grave Lantern, Chitin Carapace and more.
