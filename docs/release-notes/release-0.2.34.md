**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.34**

## Six new relics (suggested on Discord)
- **Dark Lotus** (500): once per Duel, at the start of your first Standby Phase, Special Summon 1 monster from your Deck or Extra Deck, ignoring its Summoning conditions.
- **Soul Fusion** (120): once per Duel, Fusion Summon a monster from your Extra Deck by banishing its materials from your GY. It cannot attack the turn it is Summoned.
- **Ultimate Armory** (130): while a monster you control is equipped with 3 or more Equip Spells, it cannot be destroyed by card effects.
- **Ring of Defense** (110): after each Duel, recover the LP you lost to your own card effects and costs during it.
- **Guardian's Call** (90): once per turn, Special Summon a "Guardian" monster from your hand while you control a face-up Equip Spell that lists its name.
- **Gate Calling** (80): once per turn, when you Tribute Summon Sanga of the Thunder, Kazejin or Suijin, Special Summon one of the other two from your hand.

## New
- **Stack Duplicate Cards** (Settings › Gameplay): copies show as one card with a dot per copy. Misprints stay separate.
- **Card window:** Previous / Next buttons, swipe and arrow keys, following the deck editor's filters and sort. Tap the card image for full-size art.
- **Why can't I activate?** A greyed-out Activate explains why when tapped. Switch it off in Settings › Gameplay.
- **Open all** when you have several packs to open.
- **Master Volume** slider. At 0, or with every sound slider at 0, the game is fully silent, so your music and videos keep playing. On iPhone, game sound mixes with them.
- **Batteryman Micro-Cell** and **Batteryman Charger** join the packs with the other Batteryman cards.

## Changes
- Link Devices also shares card sleeves and their achievements.
- Draft Duels: your opponent's relics show next to their portrait.
- Tag: the Bounty List objective shows for both players and is shared by the team.
- The duel log names the summon type. GY and Extra Deck monsters show their on-field stats. Achievements can be sorted. Favourite packs from Where to Find. "Choose a Monster Zone." prompt. Drillago dev note. Cosmetics before Settings.
- Windows app: much smaller updates, F11 fullscreen and an in-game restart notice. Reinstall from Download for Windows to get it.

## Fixes
- The game wouldn't start after beating the Ultimate Collector NotUrDaddy. Saved runs load again.
- Guardian's Armory only stops your opponent's effects. Solemn relic copied by Magic Mirror no longer negates a whole chain.
- Deck editor ATK includes Spirit Sanctuary and 7 other fused relics. CPU Ultimate Offering. Millennium Puzzle pack texts. Tag end screen scrolls on phones. Readable selection hints.
- Jar of Greed: its random free relic could land on the relic you were buying.
- The Rainbow sleeve no longer keeps the phone busy. Download for Windows no longer opens and closes a tab.
