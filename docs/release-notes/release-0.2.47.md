**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.47**

## 10K survey: the results are in
- The survey is closed. Thank you for every vote! Play the Survey Quiz: guess what players picked, game-show style, and share your score. Then see the full results, with a world map of where everyone plays.

## 2v2 Draft
- Host a 2v2 room for four players: drag players into teams, draft together and duel as a team.
- Tag and Draft also get a Random character button.

## Shop categories rework
- Archetype shelves now focus on their own cards, and broad shelves (Types, Attributes, Spells, Traps…) follow your Deck.
- Each opponent shows one relic guaranteed in the next shop.
- New shelves: Staple Spells, Staple Monsters, LV, Tribute Summons, Spell Counters, Tokens, Deck searchers, Blue-Eyes, Ninja, Chaos and Ojama.

## Loadout changes
- Serenity: new loadout Zera's Sanctuary with The Sanctuary in the Sky, Warrior of Zera and Archlord Zerato, and the new relic Herald of Heaven. Saintly Fusion is now her main loadout, and her pack adds every Sanctuary card and more Fairy support.
- Mokuba starts with Thunder Dragon ×2 and Snatch Steal. His pack adds Twin-Headed Thunder Dragon and Zaborg.
- Dartz starts with The Seal of Orichalcos.
- Duke starts with Skull Dice.
- Every main loadout now has its own name.

## Challenge Levels: shared starts
- Once 3 of your duelists have beaten a Challenge Level, every duelist can start there (up to Level 5). Their colours still change only when they beat it.

## More variety in duels
- Every opponent has their own music.
- Every duelist can now be met in duels 7 and 8 with their Tier 3 deck.
- New portraits for Valon, Strings and Alister.

## Changes
- An attack arrow stays on the board until the attack ends.
- Collection › Packs: search a card name to find the packs that hold it. Pack windows list every Type and a Staples filter.
- Deck editor: optional Attribute and Type counts (Settings › Display).
- Free Duels: delete custom decks.
- Ultimate Collector: Unready while siding, Limited marks on your deck, and Monster / Spell / Trap counts.
- Tag victories pay 25% more coins.
- Relic Seal below Challenge Level 2 seals half of your relics (rounded up).
- Name Changer: picking Toon makes them Toon monsters, and its archetype counts for Magician Staff and card previews.
- Faulty Printer also misprints free Random Packs.
- Randomized cards: "cannot control more monsters" effects send the extra monsters to the GY.
- Link devices: join another device's link anytime; shows the link ID and last sync.
- CPU: attacks directly with Jinzo #7 and Toons, and past an unattackable Legendary Fisherman; better Waboku, Negate Attack, Newdoria and Tribute choices.
- Legion the Fiend Jester's summon choice works with one tap.
- Parting Gift has its own art. The Easy button is easier to spot.

## Fixes
- The CPU's Obelisk no longer Tributes itself.
- Double Spell copying Scapegoat Summons the Sheep Tokens.
- Loyalty no longer keeps a monster you took with Enemy Controller.
- Hot Deck no longer causes an "effect went wrong" error.
- Stars Aligned no longer asks when your field is full.
- Ritual Scar shows the right ATK during the Duel.
- Resuming an old Tag or Draft room no longer rolls back your Challenge Levels.
- Roulette Spider and other Quick-Play, Field and Counter cards show their real icons.
- The ATK sort works on a new run's first deck screen.
- Randomized Standby Phase burn cards can be used, and "…, then …" effects no longer need the second part to be possible.
