**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.43**

## New duelist
- Johnny Steps: a new Tier 1 opponent with Kyky's Fusion and Equip deck: Musician King, Empress Judge, Fusion Weapon and Re-Fusion.

## New cards
- The Moth LV line: Moth LV1, LV2, LV6, LV8 and Cocoon of Evolution - S, a Moth that levels up each Standby Phase. They join every pack the original Moths are in.

## New relics
- Sphinx relics: Pyramid of Lightning, Bond of Egypt and Shadow of Anubis, plus the fused relic Chimera Sphinx.
- Insect relics: Moth Brood leaves a Moth Token behind your Insects, plus the fused relic Insect Swarm. Chitin Carapace now gives 800 DEF.
- Sealed Fate: your Destiny Board and Spirit Message cards cannot be targeted by card effects.
- Restless Dead now works in your opponent's Standby Phase. Narrow Gate now closes the outer zones.

## Drag to play, smoother
- Drop a Trap on your field and it's Set. A card with only one way to play just plays. The card waits on its zone while you choose, and you can drag cards along your hand to reorder it. Any hand card can be picked up and played with.

## New
- New portraits: 38 character portraits replaced with new ones, and every character pack rebuilt with them.
- Completion: the title screen shows how complete your game is, counted from your achievements.
- New settings: Spin/Slots Effects (turn off the spinning reels), Original Japanese Names for duelists (cards keep theirs), Auto-Pick Single Choices (on by default). Auto-Placement is now on by default.
- Auto-fill: short of the minimum? The grey arrow next to the card count shows which cards would fill your deck, and adds them with one tap.
- Tag duels: the Trade button now works in the shop's deck editor, and shopping waits while a trade is open.
- Draft: view your deck while you pick cards and while you wait for your opponent. The waiting room shows both players' names and relics, and the Draft deck editor shows your relics too.

## Changes
- The shop tour points at each part with a hand and dims the rest.
- Life Points stop at 10,000,000 and show ∞.
- Fusion hints also list fused relics you haven't found yet, as ??? silhouettes.
- Deck Spyglass lists Monsters, Spells, Traps, then the Extra Deck.
- Ruling notes on Harpie Lady, Ritual Monsters, Man-Eater Bug and more.
- Lighter Duel Look tints, so animated backgrounds show through.
- Harpie's Pet Dragon is much more common in Mai's pack.
- The payout shows the minimum for an Exodia or Final Countdown win.
- Link codes for your devices last 24 hours.
- CPU: uses the same card at most 3 times a turn, and plays Waboku, Painful Choice, The Shallow Grave, Re-Fusion and Fusion Weapon better.
- Solomon is a little chattier in portrait mode.

## Fixes
- Linux app: no longer stuck on "Starting campaign runtime".
- Windows app: a clear message when a firewall blocks online play.
- Tag rooms no longer say they're full when you were the one who joined.
- You can attack again under Gravekeeper's Servant with The Third Gate.
- Dimensional Rift also banishes Set cards.
- Long card lists no longer hide the ✓ / ✕ buttons, so Omni Banishment can be skipped.
- Your opponent's Painful Choice shows the cards you pick from.
- Locked zones no longer carry into the next duel.
- Drop Off asks on Chain: Sometimes.
- The CPU no longer heals into Bad Reaction to Simochi.
- DNA Rewrite: cards in your hand show their new Type's bonuses.
- Draft duels without relics no longer repeat their first dice rolls.
- A run stuck on its last duel continues again.
