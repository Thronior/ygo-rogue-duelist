**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.44**

## New cards
- Plant support: 10 Plant cards join Savage Wilds, including Lonefire Blossom, Gigaplant, Tytannial and The World Tree. Weevil's new loadout Overgrown Garden replaces Insect Evolution.
- Joey's pack: Silent Swordsman LV3, LV5, LV7, Red-Eyes Black Metal Dragon and Metalmorph. Gearfried the Iron Knight is easier to find.

## Easy mode
- Starting a run asks for a difficulty. Easy: no boss curses, heal 2,000 LP after each win, extra removal cards at the start, and shadow relics can be skipped. Easy runs don't count for achievements or unlocks.

## Relics
- Plant relics: Photosynthesis heals for your Plants, Spring Growth grows them each turn, and Pollinator brings Moth Tokens.
- Destiny Hourglass now offers the Destiny Board pieces you're still missing first.
- Banish relics: tap the ✕ on a relic in the shop to banish it for 10 coins. It won't show up again this run. Works in Tag too.
- Once per Duel relics: when one triggers, its art shows big, and its icon in the relics bar greys out for the rest of the Duel.
- 3 new fused relics to discover.

## Online
- Ultimate Collector: each play adds 15 seconds to your clock. Running out loses that duel, not the match. The timer turns red under a minute. Ask for a pause (2 per match). See your own deck on the prize screen, and mark cards with ★ / ✕.
- Draft: each pick now opens 3 packs, and every loop starts with 3 packs from 3 different sets.
- Online play, stats and replays need an official release from now on. Older versions keep working until December 1 while you update.

## New
- CPU Stats: a new tab with every CPU's wins and losses against players, split by tier. Tap a CPU to see its record against each duelist. Anonymous: no player is tracked.
- Settings › Hotkeys: every shortcut can be rebound. CPU Action Delay slows the CPU down, from 0 to 1,000 ms per action.
- Deck building: search updates as you type, the split deck view has an Extra Deck pane, and Collection › Packs can filter by Draw, Equip Spells and monster Types.
- New god sleeves: Slifer, Obelisk, Ra, Yami Yugi, Seto Kaiba and Yami Marik.
- 10,000 players! A short survey pops up once: your favourite duelists, opponents and relics, and what you'd like to see next. You can find it again under the bell.

## Changes
- A dice button on the character select picks a random duelist.
- The title screen shows achievements done / total, with the percent in the bar.
- CPU: plays about 30 more cards in Tier 4 and Collector decks, weighs board wipes by total ATK, and handles King Tiger Wanghu, Patrician of Darkness and Breaker better.
- Randomized cards are more varied, with fewer straight copies of real effects.
- Fusion and Equip filters find every Fusion Monster and Equip Spell.
- Your Graveyard opens like the Deck: the first tap shows Inspect.
- The Settings ✕ takes you back where you came from. Esc closes any window.
- Fusion hints count the pieces a fused relic needs.
- Linux app: missing sound codecs no longer stop the game, and a notice names the packages to install.

## Fixes
- Nutrient Z counts Hollow Chalice's extra damage.
- Singleton Pact removes extra copies from your Deck when you get it.
- Loyalty also protects face-down monsters, for both Tag players.
- Tag: boss curses no longer stack.
- Attack and Receive asks on Chain: Sometimes.
- Turning off Coin Reward Pop-ups also hides the +coins pop-ups in duels.
- Deck editor: taps no longer stop working, and the game says so if it can't save on your device.
- A duel no longer freezes when an animation stalls.
- Randomized cards: costs paid from the hand work, and clearer wording.
- Behaviour flags: Black Pendant, piercing damage, Last Will and Archfiend General.
- Linux app: sounds no longer cut off early.
- The Annoy Grandpa Solomon sleeve is given to everyone who did.
