**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.28**

## Favourite packs
Tap ☆ on any booster pack in the shop (solo and Tag), in Collection › Packs or in its details. Favourites go first on the shelf, the shop shows how many are in stock, and Collection gets a ★ Favourites filter.

## The CPU plays like you
10,662 player turns were replayed to find the combo lines players use that the CPU missed. It now plays these five:
- **Change of Heart** takes your lone Set monster when that attack wins the duel.
- **Sonic Bird, Senju and Manju** search a Ritual Spell, then go straight into the Ritual Summon.
- **Cannon Soldier** is summoned from the hand to burn you for game.
- **Heavy Storm, MST** and friends clear your Set Spells and Traps before a lethal attack.
- **Dark Master - Zorc** rolls its die when a 1–5 would win, and never when its attacks already do.

## FPS cap and text size
A new **FPS Cap** slider in Settings › Display (30–120). Lower caps use less battery. The game never goes above 120 FPS. The **Text Size** slider now scales all the text in the game, not just a few labels.

## Language flags
The language picker shows real flag images, so Korean and Chinese look right everywhere.

## Sort and search
Character Stats and the relic stats in Global Stats can be sorted: tap any column, tap again to reverse. A search box finds a character or relic by name.

## New relic art
25 more relics get new artwork.

## Readable buttons
Text inside buttons is now easy to read in every display style: small lines like "Completed", highlighted tabs, and greyed-out buttons.

## New & Changes
- **Shop relics:** the first two relic slots favour relics that suit your run; the third and any extra slots are now fully random.
- **Duel screen (phones):** the field sits a little lower, giving the card info panel more room; on short screens the zones keep their shape.
- **Deck editor:** your Sort and Deck status choices are kept between duels and runs.
- **Shop (phones):** the top bar stays on one line, and your coins no longer overlap the next-duel button.
- **Main menu:** long card titles no longer get cut off.

## Balance
- **Sun and Moon Dial:** your monsters gain 200 ATK during your turn and 200 DEF during your opponent's turn (was 100).
- **Opponent decks:** replaced Magic Cylinder in 4 decks.

## Fixes
- **Shadow of Eyes:** Chain Sometimes now asks when your opponent Sets a monster.
- **Amazoness Chain Master** shows the opponent's whole hand, not only their monsters.
- **Cloud save:** Level 6 clears now sync to linked devices, with the rainbow portrait.
