v0.1.81

Gameplay & Quality of Life
- You can now open Options and inspect cards during your opponent's turn. Options pauses single-player duels until closed.
- Added Pause When Tabbed Out in Settings, enabled by default.
- Inspect booster contents on opponent and boss selection screens.
- Completing Challenge LVL -1 also unlocks character variants. Previous completed runs count.
- The Normal Monster-only unlock now accepts any monster-only Main Deck.

Shop Improvements
- Redesigned the relic-only shop for Copycat and Dueling Engine, with Solomon and the controls above the relics in portrait and landscape.
- Removed unusable card-stock and pack-related relics from both characters' shop pools.
- Dueling Engine keeps Travel Satchel for its extra reward-deck choice.
- Copycat no longer receives card-reward rolls after victories. Coin rewards are unchanged.

CPU Improvements
- Refined discard and resource decisions, including Magic Drain, Dark Coffin, and recovery effects.
- Improved hand-limit discards when holding Tribute Monsters without enough available Tributes.
- Improved chain-response selection and Mirage of Nightmare removal timing.
- The CPU prefers placing cards closer to the center, while equally rated choices retain some variation.

Visuals
- Standard Pegasus now uses Relinquished as his portrait background.
- Various minor UI tweaks.
