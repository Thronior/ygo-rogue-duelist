**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.5**

**New relics**
- **Ten-thousand Hands**: a random Ritual Monster or Ritual Spell from your Deck to your starting hand. Pegasus now starts with it.
- **Union Hangar**: a random Union monster to your starting hand.
- **Restless Dead**: with no monsters at your Standby Phase, revives a random Zombie from your GY in Defense Position.
- **Kindred Bond**: +100 ATK/DEF per other face-up monster of the same Type.
- **Overcharge**: Thunder monsters gain 200 ATK per other face-up Thunder.

**Characters** (thanks Muniqui for the spreadsheet!)
- New second starts: Noah **Spirit Ascension**, Mako **Penguin Patrol**, Marik **Egyptian Gods**, Duke **Dark Scorpion Gang**, Yami Marik **Inferno**, Espa Roba **Power Grid**.
- Retuned second starts for Yami Yugi, Seto, Bonz, Weevil, Tea, Lumis & Umbra and Mokuba (new relics and packs).
- Anubis now starts with Pyramid of Light, Andro Sphinx, Sphinx Teleia and Boss Monster.
- Buffs: Millennium Shield +600 DEF (was 250), Golden Scale 30% of held coins up to 90 (was 10%, max 30), Dragon's Claws 500 damage (was 300).

**Packs**
- Pegasus's pack reworked: Exodia, Serket and Temple of the Kings out; Ritual, Dark Magician and Gravekeeper support in, with rare staples like Dark Master - Zorc, Dark Paladin, Change of Heart and Magic Cylinder.
- Polymerization is twice as likely in 10 fusion-heavy character packs.
- New cards for Lumis & Umbra, Shadi, Yami Marik and Espa Roba.

**Menus**
- Screens and windows fade in, buttons press down when tapped, and hover and keyboard focus are clearer. Lighter in Performance Mode and with reduced motion.

**Fixes**
- **Copy image** on the run card now works on Android (clipboard + share menu) and no longer opens a share window on desktop. On itch.io, right-click the card → Copy image.
- Friend runs: the shop Reroll button now shows on desktop.
- Graveyard and banished cards show their live stats.
- Cursed relic screen uses deck and relic icons; tidier run card.
