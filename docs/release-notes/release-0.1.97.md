**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.1.97**

**Duel & CPU**
- The CPU now accounts for the Mage relic’s Spellcaster ATK bonus when choosing summon position and avoids attacks into lethal Reflect Bounder damage.
- The CPU considers Sword & Shield and the standard ATK/DEF swap curse when choosing whether to summon or set a monster.
- CPU duel decks are shuffled for each duel, with seeded shuffles preserved in replays.
- Thin Ranks now removes 2 cards from the starting hand normally and 3 when amplified.

**Watch CPU Duel**
- Added Test Duel to the deck editor. It uses the deck as currently edited, including a blank deck, and lets you choose an opponent’s deck.
- Adding cards no longer resets the deck editor’s scroll position.

**Relics & Curses**
- Bounty List now awards a random 50–120 coins for each completed bounty, with the payout shown on the objective banner.
- Damage Echo no longer retriggers Dark Room of Nightmare, preventing their damage loop.

**Shop**
- Fixed the Android Relics button occasionally ignoring the first tap when entering a shop.
- Solomon now anchors to the top of the relic shelf in the desktop shop layout.

**Pack Opening**
- Super Rare cards now have their own rarity label and reveal treatment.

**Character Packs**
- Added the Dartz Character pack.
