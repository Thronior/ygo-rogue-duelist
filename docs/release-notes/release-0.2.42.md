**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.42**

## Linux app (experimental)
- A Linux desktop app, like the Windows one: a small launcher that downloads the game once, then only the updates, and updates itself. Find it under Download the app (`YGO-Rogue-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz`).

## Drag to play
- Drag a card from your hand up onto your field to Summon, Set or activate it. The zone you drop it on is where it goes. Tapping still works as before.

## New duelists
- Bobasa: a new Tier 1 opponent with Kyky's Lamp and Jar deck: La Jinn, Ancient Lamp and a very unpredictable Dice Jar.
- Gozaburo Kaiba: a new Tier 1 opponent with Nori's Exodia Necross deck: he fills his Graveyard with Exodia pieces to power Necross up.

## Toon support
- Toon Dark Magician, Toon Briefcase, Toon Mask and Comic Hand join Pegasus's packs.
- New relic Toon Premiere: at the start of each Duel, place 1 "Toon World" from your Deck face-up in your Spell & Trap Zone.
- New fused relic Toon Kingdom: Toon World from your Deck, protected from card effects, and your Toons gain 500 ATK and can attack the turn they are Summoned.

## Relic changes
- Tribute Free and Titan's Covenant: Level 7 or higher monsters you Tribute Summon require 1 less Tribute, so the Egyptian Gods now need 2.
- Living Traps: start the Duel with a random Trap Monster in hand; your Trap Monsters can't be destroyed by your opponent's Spell/Trap effects (instead of by battle).
- Level Up Chain: your LV monsters are unaffected only by your opponent's monster effects, so their own effects work again.
- Spell Provider and Spell Counter Engine place the counter by themselves when only one card can take it.
- Unstable DNA remembers your last Type and Attribute.
- The shadow relic Reversed Remedy is offered less often.
- Draft Duels: Stars Aligned and 13 other relics now work for whoever owns them, and "both players" relics hit both.
- Fused relics that replaced an archetype relic keep its shop bonus.

## Smarter CPU
- Learned from your replays: it trades equal-ATK monsters, plays Susa Soldier and Asura Priest properly, stops Tributing monsters that could still attack, and plays the World Championship deck's combos.
- Limiter Removal waits for the Battle Phase, Dust Tornado skips empty lock cards, Dark Spirit of the Silent picks your weakest monster, and the CPU picks a random hat against Magical Hats.

## New
- Fusion hints: once you've discovered a fused relic, its pieces show which fusion they're part of and how many you own.
- Shop tour: your first visit to the shop starts a short guided tour. Tap ? in the shop to see it again.
- Watch CPU duel has a My decks tab for your own saved decks, and custom decks have a name you can change.
- Full animations for everyone: only the game's own Reduce Motion setting turns animations down, not your device's setting.

## Changes
- Locked zones are crossed out, whatever locks them.
- The deck editor shows your Monster, Spell, Trap and Extra Deck counts.
- ★ All marks or clears every card in your current search at once.
- New settings: Skip pack animations and Summon Cut-ins.
- Android: lock the screen to portrait or landscape in Settings.
- Randomized cards say ??? RANDOMIZED ??? when you inspect them.
- The Coin scorecard explains the hidden bonuses you've found.
- Shorter setting descriptions, and UI Scale now goes down to 50%.
- The payout shows its multipliers, like Glass Shard ×3 or the Challenge level penalty −25%.
- Randomizer: 200 cards reviewed and about 40 kinds of wording fixed.
- Some secrets are a little more forgiving now.

## Fixes
- The UI Scale slider slides back below 100% and starts at the right spot.
- Menus, tooltips and dragged cards line up at any UI Scale.
- Tag: your partner's Type choice window (DNA Rewrite) closes by itself, and cards from free Random Packs can be inspected.
- The Selection and other Summon-negating Traps ask on Chain sometimes.
- Card pictures no longer go missing in the card window (translated card art).
- Randomized texts are no longer half-translated, and two randomized texts now match what the card does.
