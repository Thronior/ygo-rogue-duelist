**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.17**

**New Next-Opponent Screen**
- All 5 opponents at a glance: portrait, shop boost and guaranteed pack in one row, on every screen shape.
- Shop boosts show their Type, Attribute or effect icon. Crossed swords start the duel.
- The header stays on one line and is thinner on phones.

**8 Duel Backgrounds**
- Every opponent now duels on 1 of 8 animated backgrounds: Spiral, Summoning Circle, Star Drift, Ember Rise, Aurora, Ripples, Light Rays and Glyph Rain.
- They keep playing over Field Spell art. The Tunnel background is gone.

**New Relic**
- Hot Deck (100 coins): each time your opponent draws, they also send that many cards from the top of their Deck to the GY.

**6 New Curses**
- Effect Reverser: for both players, effect damage heals and healing hurts. Level 2+: only what you do to your opponent is flipped.
- Shield Breaker: you can't Set monsters, except by Special Summon. Level 2+: you can't Summon or Set in Defense Position.
- Relic Mirror: your opponent gets 2 random relics you have. Level 2+: they copy all of them.
- Hand Limiter: your hand size limit drops by 2, or 3 at Level 2+. Stacks.
- No Specials Allowed: neither player can Special Summon. Level 2+: only you can't.
- Miller: once per duel, send the top 10 cards of your Deck to the GY. Level 2+: the top 50%.

**Characters**
- Shadi starts with the Millennium Shield relic, Sengenjin, Millennium Golem and Millennium Shield.
- New Shadi second loadout, Sands of Judgment: Hot Deck with Morphing Jar #2, Needle Worm and Book of Taiyou. It replaces Ritual Judgment.
- Shadi's pack gains mill and opponent-draw cards, and loses its ritual and Dark Magician support.
- Yugi Muto starts with the Sun and Moon Dial relic.

**Changes**
- Deck editor: phones in portrait show 5 cards per row.

**Fixes**
- Tag: a guest using Tribe-Infecting Virus or another card that declares a monster Type no longer freezes the duel.
