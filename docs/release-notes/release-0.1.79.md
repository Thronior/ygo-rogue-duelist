## Performance Improvements
- Reduced memory growth during long play sessions.
- Reduced background processing and unnecessary save writes. Duels now autosave at phase changes.
- Added an optional Performance Mode for lighter visuals on mobile.

## New Relics
- **Dice Jar → 100 Coins →** Gain 1 free reroll in each shop and 1 additional reroll for each cursed relic choice.
- **Morphing Jar #2 → 100 Coins →** Shop rerolls restock your character's booster pack, allowing you to buy it again.
- **Cyber Jar → 100 Coins →** Gain 1 free opponent reveal per encounter and 1 free boss reroll per boss encounter.
- **Solemn Relic → 150 Coins →** Once per duel, negate your opponent's first Spell, Trap, or monster-effect activation that can be negated.

## New Card
- **Ancient Treasure → Normal Spell →** Receive 50 Coins.
- Available in All Spells packs and general shop stock.
