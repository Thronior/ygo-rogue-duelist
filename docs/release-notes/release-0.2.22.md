**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.22**

## Controller support
Play with an Xbox, PS4 or PS5 controller. A cursor shows your selection, and each screen shows its buttons.
- **D-pad / left stick:** move the cursor. **Right stick:** scroll.
- **A / Cross:** select, like a click. **B / Circle:** back, like a right click.
- **X / Square:** inspect, like a long press. **Y / Triangle:** jump to the phase buttons in duels.
- **LB / RB, Start, Select:** shortcuts for chain mode, buy, reroll, next duel and settings.

## Fused relic changes
- **Masterwork Blade:** buffed.
- **Second Coin Toss:** reworked.
- **Commoner's Crown:** nerfed.

## New & Changes
- **Deck editor:** new deck status filter (Deck first by default), a reset button, bigger in-deck cards and Deck Card Size up to 20. Auto-build was removed. All signature cards start in your deck.
- **In-duel Settings:** change your Duel Look mid-duel. Settings are sorted into tabs.
- **Millennium Puzzle packs:** Green holds every Spell, Red every Trap, Yellow every monster, Purple every card.
- **Global Stats:** tap a relic to see what it does. Fused relics show their Fusion Material once discovered.
- **Chain Sometimes:** ATK/DEF Traps like Castle Walls ask in the Damage Step, so they can target a flipped monster.
- **Rafael's** pack gains Soul Charge.
- **Dueling Engine:** new look on character select.
- **Opponent screen:** new Back to shop button.
- **Reward reels:** improved animation.
- **Relic Mirror (amplified)** copies 4 random relics instead of all of them.

## Smarter CPU
- Viser Des isn't tributed while it's locking a target.
- Face-up Flip monsters like Cyber Jar aren't switched to Attack for nothing.
- Magical Dimension summons its strongest Spellcaster and skips the destroy with no targets.

## Fixes
- Relic Mirror at LVL 5+ no longer hands you the opponent's own relic and buffs.
- The Relic Fusion reveal plays right away, also when a cursed relic is the last piece.
- Character select no longer flickers when picking a duelist, and card inspections close cleanly.
