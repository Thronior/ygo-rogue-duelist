**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.1.98**

**Duel & CPU**
- The CPU now tracks both decks and avoids optional draws or self-mill when they risk losing by deck-out. It accounts for draw engines, revival effects, Cyber Jar, Nimble Momonga, and Bubonic Vermin.
- Improved the CPU's use of Back to Square One, Good Goblin Housekeeping, multi-card choices, and discard costs while protecting useful combo pieces.
- Improved Megarock Dragon and Egyptian God Card evaluation, including Slifer's hand-based ATK and Ra's actual LP-based effects. The CPU now recognizes Obelisk's board wipe and uses Sanctuary in the Sky when its field zone is empty.
- The CPU uses Snatch Steal when it can make a meaningful immediate difference, and avoids giving the opponent LP when that outweighs the stolen monster.

**Opponents & Decks**
- Added Seeker as a Tier 3 opponent with the Magnet Warrior and Rock deck.
- Replaced Princess Adena's Tier 3 deck with the supplied Sanctuary in the Sky / Fairy deck.
- Added Obelisk the Tormenter as a Tier 4 opponent with the supplied 52-card stall deck, including Heavy Storm and Harpie's Feather Duster. This deck ignores the Limited List.
- After the second completed campaign run, Obelisk becomes the loop challenger in place of the World Champion.

**Shop**
- Fixed shop items sometimes ignoring the first tap after entering the shop.
- Solomon now scales to the screen, stays anchored to the relic shelf, and is capped at half the screen height on desktop.
