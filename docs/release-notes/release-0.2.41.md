**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.41**

## Drag to attack
- In your Battle Phase, drag your monster onto the one you want to attack, or onto an empty field for a direct attack. Tapping still works as before.

## 22 new taunts
- I convinced my brother and a friend to come up with and record some taunts, so losing a duel now plays one of 22 new "you lose" lines at random.

## New decks to face
- Guardian Angel Joan: a new Tier 3 opponent with Kyky's LIGHT Fairy deck.
- PaniK now shows up early too, with two new Fiend / Yami decks by Deds for Tier 1 and Tier 2.

## Two new fused relics
- My Pride and Soul: Kaiba's answer to Immortal Dark Magic. Dragons gain 800 ATK/DEF, no Traps in your Battle Phase, a Blue-Eyes from your Deck at the start of the Duel, and a LIGHT Dragon to replace a fallen Blue-Eyes.
- Unstable DNA: at the start of the Duel, pick a Type and an Attribute. Every monster of both players becomes them, and yours gain ATK for each match on the field.

## Stronger character packs
- Serenity's pack now has staples: Pot of Greed, Graceful Charity, Monster Reborn, Raigeki, Mirror Force and more.
- The Paradox Brothers get staples and ways to take a monster to Tribute (Change of Heart, Snatch Steal, Brain Control, Enemy Controller).
- Both lose filler.

## Ultimate Collector, upgraded
- The Ultimate Collector deck builder now works like the solo one: search window, type tabs, stacked copies, sorting and the wide-screen split.
- Card search everywhere also finds cards by their name and text in your language.

## Changes
- Favourite packs are now saved per character; the star lets you pick which ones.
- The Tag shop offers both players' character packs.
- Tapping a relic in the duel's relic bar opens the full Run relics window.
- When a relic asks you something, the prompt shows which relic is asking.
- Healing relics show up more often when your LP is low.
- Broken Limiter shows up more often the more copies of one card you own.
- Sleeves are grouped with their alternate arts, with an Unlocked first option.
- The shadow relic screen shows your VICTORIES.
- Grandpa now stands in Copycat's and Dueling Engine's shop on phones, and has something to say when there's nothing to sell.
- Randomized cards read better (no more repeated names), and "loses ATK/DEF" effects work.
- Dimension Jar is its original print: it banishes monsters only.
- The CPU plays Fairy decks better, keeps Level Up! going upward, stops reviving Gearfried with Equips and no longer heals itself into damage under Reversed Remedy.

## Fixes
- In-app updates work on busy networks and in China, with clearer error messages.
- Behaviour flags from very long runs upload again.
- On the web, a full browser storage now clears card pictures to keep saving your progress, and the save error is translated.
- "On the field" stats are right when an ATK/DEF swap curse meets a swap relic.
- A relic-boosted Spirit Reaper attacks instead of hiding in Defense.
- Face-down monsters count for "win while you control…" sleeve goals.
- Randomized Fusions no longer ask for two different sets of materials.
