**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.27**

## Union relics
New relic **Unionize**: your monsters gain 300 ATK for each Union monster equipped to them. A new fused relic, **Union Buster**, also joins the game.

## Fused relics buffed
**Harpie Lady Sisters' Nest**, **Amazoness Village**, **Necrovalley Throne** and **Don Zaloog's Hideout** are stronger. **Endless Archive** is now called **Divine Archive**.

## New relic art
15 more relics get new artwork, including Boss Monster, Relic Picker, Weighted Coins and the Token relics.

## Better translations
Every language got a terminology review against the official Yu-Gi-Oh! and big game translations. Card types, duel actions and shop buttons are now translated, and long words no longer overflow.

## Faster and cooler
The Android app starts faster, and the deck editor uses less battery.

## New & Changes
- **Spells and Traps** list Activate first.
- **Shop (desktop):** Edit deck sits further away from Reroll.
- **Main menu** card names shrink to fit one line.
- **Relic Picker** can offer very rare relics, as it always could; its text no longer says otherwise.
- **itch.io in Brave:** a warning explains that Brave deletes saves there, and how to keep yours.

## Smarter CPU
- Against **Ordeal of a Traveler**, the CPU no longer always calls Trap.

## Fixes
- **Nutrient Z** now counts relics and curses that raise the damage you take.
- **Deck editor and shop:** fixed a state where cards could no longer be added or inspected until a restart.
- **Webapp:** the browser is asked to keep your save permanently.
