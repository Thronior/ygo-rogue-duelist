**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.3**

**New opponents & decks**
- New T1 opponent: **Ojama King**.
- 16 new CPU decks from #cpu-decks, balanced to 40–60% in their tier: Darkbishop Archfiend T1, Breaker T1/T3, Cecelia Pegasus T1–T3, Johnson T1/T3, Mokuba T1/T3, Obelisk the Tormenter T1/T2, Yugi Muto T1/T2 (Level Friends) and Princess Adena T1/T2.

**CPU**
- Fixed 11 misplays found in your replays: Kelbek, Enemy Controller timing, Hane-Hane bouncing its best monster, pointless attacks into equal DEF, Temple of the Kings, removal ignoring double attackers, missed lethal summons, ATK equips on Defense monsters, Cyber Jar order, Dragon Capture Jar and Patrician of Darkness.
- About 40 cards the CPU never used now get played, incl. LV monsters like Horus and Armed Dragon, Union monsters and XYZ fusions, Spell Counter cards, Divine Wrath, Draining Shield, Cursed Seal, Anti-Spell and The Dragon's Bead.
- Ra takes its LP boost, and "already attacked" checks finally work.

**Relics**
- Surgery relics: the first one changes your monsters' Attribute, each further one adds another. Works in hand, Deck, GY and on the field.
- Misprints: the shop notice shows changed Types and Attributes, misprinted cards show their Attribute and Type icons in the deck editor, and misprints only roll Types that exist in the card pool.

**Performance**
- APK 330 → 209 MB, web 386 → 234 MB, far less memory use, and faster startup, collection screen and CPU turns. The first Android update after this is a big download.
- Web: offline files are stored once, so updates only download what changed and old copies are cleaned up (fixes "Progress could not be saved"). Saves always keep free space, and a finished update shows **Update ready · Restart**.

**Online**
- Fewer server requests: idle lobbies, Draft Duels and Ultimate Collector poll less often.
- Tag duels, Draft Duels and Ultimate Collector now need 0.2.3 or newer. Stats, device sync and backups keep working on older versions.
