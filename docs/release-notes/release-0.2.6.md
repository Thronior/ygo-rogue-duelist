**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.6**

**New**
- **Coin scorecard** in the shop is redesigned: headline numbers, a pace track for fast-win bonuses and slow-duel penalties, category tabs, and every milestone with its tiers. Gold marks what you earned last duel.
- **Shop** singles show how many copies you own (1/3, gold at 3/3). Thanks Muniqui!
- **Run card**: cursed relics get a red border, and the boss curses you faced show as icons. New **Download** button next to Copy; on Android, Copy now just copies.
- **Jester's Coin** (cursed relic): call a coin toss each Standby Phase. Right: your monsters gain 300 ATK/DEF; wrong: they lose 300, until your next Standby Phase.

**Relics**
- Broken Seal: opponents start with 2,000 fewer LP (was 1,000).
- Prospector's Lens now always adds an ultra rare to the shop.
- Razor Ledger's text now says it uses your run's current deck minimum.

**Seto Kaiba's pack** (thanks Prunk!) is rebuilt around Dragons, Unions and Obelisk: the fish support is out; King Dragun, Divine Dragon Ragnarok, Tyrant Dragon and more Dragon support are in, plus staples and Kaiba's anime cards. 131 cards.

**CPU**
- Unity protects the monster being attacked.
- No second Enemy Controller on an attack that's already stopped.
- Mobius the Frost Monarch no longer destroys its own cards.
- Ultimate Offering waits until the Normal Summon is used.
- Rescue Cat only activates when it has a follow-up.
- No Swords of Revealing Light on top of Nightmare's Steelcage.

**Fixes**
- "On the field" stats for monsters in hand now include every relic and Field Spell.
- Tokens play their destruction animation.
- A monster flipped up and back down (Acid Trap Hole) no longer stays face-up without stats.
- Cursed relic screen: just the buttons, no box.
- Android run card titles use a proper display font.
