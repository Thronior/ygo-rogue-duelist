**YGO Rogue Duelist v0.2.31**

## Draft Duels reworked
Matches are now a longer draft run. After every duel, a **Draft Screen** offers 3 relics and 3 duelist packs.
- The **loser picks first**, then the winner takes from what's left.
- **176 relics**, cursed and fused ones too. They stack and stay active for the rest of the match.
- Each pack opens **2 boosters**. Decks start at 20 cards, then 30, then 40.
- **First to 3 wins**, then both players can continue to 6, 9… or end the match.
- Hover, hold or right-click a tile to see the relic or pack, like in the shop.

Both players need v0.2.31 for the new rules.

## Tag loops
After the final 1v1, Tag offers **Start loop 2**. When both players vote yes, you head back to the shop together for a harder loop.

## Clear server messages
No more bare "Failed to fetch". If the online servers hit today's usage limit, the game says so and shows when they come back (00:00 UTC). It also tells you when the servers can't be reached or you're offline. Cloud saves and global stats now write far less, so the daily limit is hit less often.

## Changes
- **Rainbow sleeve:** win Challenge Level 6 with all 36 duelists.
- See your **opponent's sleeve** in Draft Duels and Ultimate Collector; monsters you take keep their owner's sleeve.
- **Typhoon** now goes off in your opponent's End Phase.
- **Stars Aligned:** 175 → 130 coins.
- **Iron Silence** text is clearer: it negates all your monsters' activated effects everywhere.
- **Run cards:** champion wins keep their Challenge Level's frame colour.
- **Escape** and **right-click** on empty space close menus and windows.

## Fixes
- "Win with X duelists" sleeves now unlock when the achievement is done.
- **Cursed Restock:** the Reroll button shows 0 while rerolls are free.
- **Relic Mirror** no longer copies Defense Proficiency while you have Mercy.
- **German shop:** "KAUFEN" fits on the Buy button.
- Tapping a locked sleeve again replaces the message instead of stacking them.
