**YGO Rogue Duelist — Update 0.2.16**

**New Relics**
- Protection Charm (120 coins): disables 1 random curse for the duel.
- Fire Extinguisher (110 coins): negates the first effect damage from a card each turn.
- Normal Barrier (150 coins): your Normal Monsters ignore your opponent's Spell Cards.
- Tenacity of the Weak (100 coins): card effects can't destroy your Level 3 or lower Normal Monsters.
- Stand Tall (90 coins): stat reductions on your monsters are halved.
- Protect the Weak (100 coins): with 2+ monsters, your weakest can't be attacked.
- Sacrifice the Weak (100 coins): with 2+ monsters, your strongest can't be attacked.
- Token Defender (90 coins): no monsters at your End Phase? Summon a Lamb Token in Defense.
- The Creator (300 coins, rare): Special Summon 1 monster from your GY each Standby Phase.

**New Curses**
- Fireproof Vest: effect damage to your opponent is halved, or removed at Level 2+.
- Spell, Trap and Monster Jammer: activating that card type costs 300 LP, or 600 at Level 2+.

**Pre-errata**
- Necrovalley, Y-Dragon Head, Z-Metal Tank, Heavy Mech Support Platform, Hallowed Life Barrier, Weapon Change and Mind Crush now play their original effects.

**Changes**
- Every relic now has its own card art.
- Relic offers weigh Deck cards more than the rest of your collection.
- Relic Picker can now offer very rare relics.
- The Underdog Clause counts every Level 3 or lower monster.
- Open Wound raises battle damage only. Withering Soul stops at 2,000 LP, or 1,000 at Level 2+.
- CPU: new lines with Call of the Mummy, A Legendary Ocean and Soul Exchange. Tristan's T2 deck gains Twin-Headed Thunder Dragon. Ring of Destruction, Brain Control, Sinister Serpent and Night Assailant follow their pre-errata rules.

**Fixes**
- Morphing Jar #2 and other face-down or Defense summons work under Berserker's Pact.
- A failed sound or effect can no longer skip your relic pick, pack opening or card reveal.
