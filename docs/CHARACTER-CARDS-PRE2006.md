# Characters x pre-2006 cards

Every playable character and every pre-2006 card associated with them.
Sources: (1) this project — signature cards, draft themes, full CPU decklists per tier; (2) supplied research — pre-2006 physical products (OCG Structures, TCG Starters, PC promos) and video-game decks (Nightmare Troubadour 2005, WC2004, Reshef of Destruction, EDS packs).
Pre-2006 status comes from dated card data: 1848/1866 pool cards predate 2006; the 18 exceptions are listed at the end. Full contents of historical physical/game decks are associations (deck names/products), not reprinted card-by-card; full card lists below are the project's pool associations filtered to pre-2006.
