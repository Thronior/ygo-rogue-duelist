YGO Rogue Duelist 0.1.50

NEW: Draft Duels
- Pick a character, host or join a room, and keep their signature cards plus double starting boosters.
- Both players ready up before the single-player pack-opening animations begin. Build independently, then both ready up to duel.
- Best of three, 40-60 card Main Deck, no siding or banned/limited list, maximum three copies by name.
- Fresh deck shuffles each duel and automatic room reconnection.

CPU improvements
- Smarter equip targets: considers extra attacks, visible blockers, Maha Vailo's bonus and spreading equips across monsters.
- Sonic Bird, Manju and Senju search for ritual pieces that complement the current hand.
- Fixed wasteful Limiter Removal timing, unfavorable Time Wizard gambles and Temple of the Kings choosing an unsupported Serket.

Other changes
- Special Level 5 completion celebration with a golden conquest banner, lights and confetti.
- Fallen Ultimate Collector decks can appear in later single-player loops using their character portraits.
- Reward reroll buttons sit beneath their card frames.
- Mobile portrait and landscape layout fixes for Draft Duels.
